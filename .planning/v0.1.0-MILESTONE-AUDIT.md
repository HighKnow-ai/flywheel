## Verdict

The product thesis is proven end to end: the board is the store of record, every seam between phases is wired, and all three flows complete. What blocks a clean milestone is narrower than it looks:

- **Two requirements (AGNT-05, PLAN-01) need a credential-spending live run, not code.** Both are Phase 9, both are registered, both have the code path proven offline.
- **One requirement (DIST-01) needs a $99/yr purchase the owner declined.** It is unmet, unticked, and — unlike everything else here — maps to no phase in any roadmap. It needs a named home before it can be worked.
- **22 owner-questions from the redeemed review register are unanswered.** They are not blockers by the audit's own gate, but they are the milestone's real backlog.
