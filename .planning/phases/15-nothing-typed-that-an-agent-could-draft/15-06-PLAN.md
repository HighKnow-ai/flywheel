<objective>
**As a** developer who starts work on a task that has no acceptance criteria roughly always,
**I want to** be handed drafted criteria in the launch sheet I am already reading, decide each one,
and still be able to say "proceed without criteria" on the record,
**so that** the contract gets written at the moment I was already looking at the task, instead of
never.

D-10's reasoning is the load-bearing part: criteria arrive when you reach the task, not when the
epic is decomposed. Five decisions at the moment you were already opening the task, rather than
thirty at the moment you were admiring a decomposition. That is the single decision that keeps this
engine off the chore side of PITFALLS' table.

Purpose: close DRFT-02's routing clause and DRFT-10's surface, derive the judgement-only count
A-08 asks for, and run the same lint on human text as advice.
Output: one coverage read, a drafting route inside the existing launch sheet, a recorded escape
hatch, and a non-blocking warning on the manual form.
</objective>

| `task_criteria_waive` → the event log | the escape hatch's whole value is that it is attributable and countable |
