## Design finding worth settling first

The first burn attempt asked its sessions to write essay FILES. Flywheel's own FLYWHEEL-SCOPE hook
refused every Write, because the scratch task declares no file-set — so the sessions burned nothing
and went looking for Flywheel MCP tools. That is the product working correctly and the instrument
being wrong.

The deeper problem the interruption exposed: `uat-15` expects the limit to surface as a sidecar
`rate_limit` event on a burn lane, but the orchestrating agent's own API calls hit the limit first
and die. Capacity was genuinely spent and the window genuinely drained — and the instrument still
captured nothing. A burn whose own driver is the first casualty cannot record its own result.
Settle that before the next window is spent, or it will be spent for nothing again.
