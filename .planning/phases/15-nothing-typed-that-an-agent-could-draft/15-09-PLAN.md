<objective>
**As a** developer whose only datum is "1 of 42 drafted criteria was amended", which cannot tell
excellent drafting from reflex acceptance,
**I want to** see how many drafts are waiting against the cap, and what fraction of the drafts I
accepted I later revised,
**so that** the question "is this engine helping me or training me to click Accept" has a number
instead of a belief.

PITFALLS prevention 7 names the post-accept revision rate as the outcome metric and says success is
defined on it, not on criteria count. Instrument first, tune after: 30 and N are first settings with
tests on them, not tuned values, and D-27's number is what argues for changing either.

Purpose: close DRFT-08's visible-back-pressure clause and DRFT-11 entirely, both derived from the
event log rather than from new telemetry.
Output: one read producing both numbers, and one project-level region printing them side by side.
</objective>
