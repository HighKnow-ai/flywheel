<objective>
The phase's leading end-to-end slice: a user registers a local git repository, sees its rack, types
a title into the inline quick-add, and the strip appears in the Backlog band — persisted through one
transaction that wrote the projection row and appended the event together, refreshed by a delta the
core pushed rather than by the renderer trusting its own write, and still there after a relaunch.

Purpose: this is the tracer. Every architectural seam the phase depends on is exercised once, on the
one path — the fleet-wide database, the per-origin event sequence, actor attribution, the human key
allocated in the same transaction, the fractional rank, the bounded indexed read, the single adapter
seam, the push-invalidate channel, and the virtualized rack. Proving them together on one commit is
what makes the eight expansion plans safe to build horizontally on top.

Output: one working path, production-quality, and the invariant suite that keeps it honest.
</objective>

    Four decisions in `01-CONTEXT.md` are already made by the developer and are listed here for the
    record, not for re-litigation: **D-06** criteria are their own table with their own ULIDs;
    **D-10** every item carries a human-readable per-project key allocated in the same transaction as

       flagged it: epics share the five-state workflow and render as ordinary strips in their band;
       issues render in a separate workflow-less lane beneath the five bands.
    2. **Does `assignee_id` exist?** `BOARD-06` literally requires filtering by assignee, and no
       decision in `01-CONTEXT.md` defines the field. Both the researcher (`01-RESEARCH.md` Open

       `assignee_id REFERENCES actors(id)` on tasks and epics now. If it is rejected, every assignee
       affordance in `01-UI-SPEC.md` — badge, filter, `a` keybinding — must be removed from the

    3. **Priority cardinality and labels** — `01-UI-SPEC.md` assumed 5 levels for the sake of a
       concrete `0`–`4` keyboard map; `D-11` fixes the tie-break rule, not the label set.
