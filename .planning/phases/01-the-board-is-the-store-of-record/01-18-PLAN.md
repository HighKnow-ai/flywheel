<design_decision>
## Why `FilterBar` loses its `items` prop rather than merely ignoring it

`FilterBar.tsx`'s module doc comment (lines 11-14) currently documents G-01-2 as an accepted
limitation, and its `FilterBarProps.items` doc comment says the prop exists "used only to derive the
epic chip list". Once the chips come from the roster, `items` has no remaining consumer in that file.

Leaving the prop in place would be the `add-alongside` outcome the assumption-delta contract warns
against: a component that can still see the window is a component a future change can quietly re-derive
from it. Removing the prop makes the promotion structural — `FilterBar` cannot read the window even by
accident, and `tsc` (which `npm --prefix packages/desktop run build` runs) rejects any call site that
tries to hand it one. The cost is updating three call sites, all of which this plan owns.

## The one derivation that stays windowed, and why

`BoardView.tsx`'s `issue-parent` picker builds two groups: Epics and Tasks. The **Epics** group moves to
the roster in Task 2. The **Tasks** group stays windowed, because tasks are the unbounded set D-03's
windowing exists for — a "list every task" command would be precisely the prohibition this plan's
sibling (01-17) forbids. Recorded as accepted debt in 01-17's `<assumption_delta_decision>`; a per-id
parent-resolution command is what would close it, and that is a different gap.

The same applies to `parentsById`'s **task** half. Its **epic** half is merged with the roster in
Task 2 so an issue under an out-of-window epic stops reading `No parent`.

## Reversibility

Both changes are `reversible`: replacing a prop and re-pointing three derivations at a different source
are local edits behind stable interfaces, with no migration, no persisted format and no published
contract. The one `costly` decision in this gap closure (the unwindowed command itself) is recorded on
01-17 Task 1, where it is made.

## Assumption-delta

The decision, its noun, and the accepted debt are recorded once, in `01-17-PLAN.md`'s
`<assumption_delta_decision>` block. This plan **implements** the `promote` half of that decision at the
three remaining sites; it does not re-decide it. Summarised for readers of this file alone: the primary
noun is *the project's epic roster*, the decision is `promote`, and the windowed derivation is removed
rather than kept alongside — here, by deleting `FilterBar`'s `items` prop.

Sourcing the chips from the roster changes where the id came from, not how it is bound |
| T-01-18-02 | Information Disclosure | chip / option / parent-button labels | low | mitigate | Labels are the epic's `key` and `title` — user-authored board content already rendered on the row itself — never an internal id.
