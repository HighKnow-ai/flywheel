**The honest-reporting rule governs this plan more than any other.** `.claude/CLAUDE.md` is explicit:
speed changes what gets fixed, never what gets claimed. Criterion 0 is subjective by design and is
closed by the owner's own decision at plan 04's gate, not by a test. Criterion 4 is closed by the

owner, this plan says so plainly rather than rounding it up. A phase that closes with unverified
criteria says so in its own artifacts.

`{phase}-UAT.md`, do not present a verification checkpoint, and do not write a verification file that
would read as awaiting a human. Anything asserted but not observed goes into
`.planning/DEFERRED-REVIEW.md` with its truth, why it was deferred, the risk if it is wrong, and an
honest automatability answer — honest because on 2026-10-07 three of Phase 08's four
supposedly-human items turned out to be automatable.

Probe fallback: skipped — this phase has no REQ-IDs to probe (visible skip). `must_haves` derive
from SC0–SC4, the CONTEXT decisions and `08.1-VALIDATION.md`'s requirement-to-test map.

Purpose: a phase that closes on evidence, with the gaps named.
Output: a green gate, a design document written from the built product, and a register that is honest
about what nobody looked at.
