**Six brand or destructive filled actions redrawn with a 3:1 control edge and one 2px ink lead per section, the launch sheet moved onto the per-mode scrim, shadow and sheet-edge roles, the permission card's side stripe and fill replaced by an edge, and a pulse that had been silently deleted by plan 08.1-13 put back and pinned. The independent finish-reviewer did not run, and no comp exists for this group.**

## What the plan asked for, and what happened

**The pulse was gone (binding decision 4).** The brief said the pulse is the one scoped exception and "must stay scoped". Looking for the keyframes to scope, they were not in `motion.css`: plan 08.1-13 removed `@keyframes agent-pulse` and `.agent-pulse` while rewriting the displacement utility (`git show 44aa448`). Three components (`SessionSurface`, `RunsSection`, `OrphanRecoveryModal`) applied a class that animated nothing. The jsdom suite could not see it, because vitest stubs stylesheets and the component tests only assert the class name; the motion test pinned durations only. Restored as it was (opacity 1 to 0.45, 1.6s, the single easing curve, infinite), in its own commit first (`531a3ab`). `motion.test.ts` now reads the real file: the keyframes exist, touch only opacity, and no other keyframes block does. WebKit asserts the glyph's computed `animationName` is `agent-pulse`, `infinite`, with a live engine animation; under `test:browser:motion` it asserts the class is absent (omitted, not frozen) and the glyph is at opacity 1. `SessionSurface`'s own reduced-motion branch was not touched.

**No brand fills, no destructive fills (binding decision 1).** `LaunchSheet.tsx:862` is redrawn, and so are the five siblings the register did not list: `SessionSurface`'s Discard, Review changes, Advance and the composer's Send, and `PermissionRequestCard`'s Allow. The launch sheet's CHOSEN permission-mode segment was the same defect (ink on `--accent-nontext`). The Deny button of the destructive gate, the second half of the decision, was ink on `--destructive-strip`: 2.95 / 2.86:1. All are `control-edge session-action`: transparent ground, 3:1 edge, 4.5:1 ink. No `--text-on-accent` anywhere; a finish test fails if `--accent-nontext`, `--text-on-accent` or `--destructive-strip` returns to any of the five components.

**The action-versus-value device from group two.** One lead per section wears the 2px `--selection-rule` ink edge with the pixel given back by padding, so it is the same height as its neighbour (asserted in WebKit): Discard while running or Review changes once finished, Advance in an authoring run's stage controls (Discard then drops to the plain edge, so the header has ONE lead), Send in the composer, Allow on a routine card, Start Work on the sheet. Everything else is on the 1px control edge. The chosen permission mode is a VALUE, so it is not boxed: an ink edge, a 2px foot rule and weight 600. It is not a surface shift either: the selected-row surface differs from the sheet's own surface by less than 1.01.

**The permission card (threat T-08.1-47).** It is gated on a human answer, so its emphasis is unchanged in substance. Routine: Allow leads, Deny beside it. Destructive: Deny leads (reversed emphasis kept, per D-57) but as a 2px edge in `--destructive-text-rack`, with the heading "Destructive action — needs your OK", the octagon glyph and a card edge in the same role, so it is told apart from the routine card by heading, glyph, edge and position, not by saturation. The 4px destructive `border-left` (the craft floor's side-stripe ban) and the `--surface-secondary` fill are gone; the card is bounded by a 3:1 `--border-control` edge. The consequence sentences and the `Allow, just this once` label are untouched, and the allow-always action is still omitted from the destructive tree.

**Hover wash on a drawn action (found by the detector, not planned).** On the rendered HTML the detector reported `low-contrast`: the destructive text role on App.css's `--divider` hover wash is 4.2:1 in dark. That is the global hover-wash defect group three logged, but here it lands under coloured text. Every `.session-action` now hovers on the board row's inset `--row-surface-hover` wash (the destructive text role on it is well over 4.5), and the destructive lead keeps its destructive edge while hovered. WebKit asserts both. The global rule in `App.css` is still untouched; plan 20 owns it.

**Overlay rule (binding decision 3).** `LaunchSheet` used `rgba(0,0,0,0.45)` and `0 16px 48px rgba(0,0,0,0.5)`: a dark-tuned scrim on a white ground. It is now `.launch-scrim` (`--scrim`) over `.launch-sheet` (`--shadow-sheet`, a `--border-sheet` edge, `--radius-chrome`). WebKit resolves the scrim to `rgba(31, 35, 40, 0.4)` light and `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)` dark, the shadow to the sheet role in each, and the edge to `#7a828c` light and `#a6a6a6` dark, and asserts frame one is at opacity 1. The sheet keeps its inline `width: 640` and `max-height: 80vh`, which `launch-sheet.test.tsx` pins.

**Focus-ring-on-prop (binding decision 2).** Checked every component in the group: none sets an inline `outline`, so none can leave a brand rectangle after a click. A finish test fails if one does, and WebKit asserts a mouse click on a feed disclosure row leaves `outline-style: none`.

**Text inputs (binding decision 5).** The group has no `input` element, only three `textarea`s (the composer, the launch sheet's Instruction and Premise). `App.css` has a focus rule for `input` and `button` and none for `textarea`, so they had been falling through to the engine's own ring, a system colour no token owns. They are now `control-edge session-field` with a 2px `--selection-rule` ring: the same reasoning as the palette, because the launch sheet's field takes focus the moment the sheet opens, so a brand box there would be a standing one. Asserted in WebKit (solid, 2px, the ink role). The global `input:focus-visible` ring is still the brand for ordinary `input`s elsewhere, and I did not touch it.

**Boundary roles.** The three textareas and four mode segments drew their edge from `--divider` (1.2 to 1.4:1); they now draw it from `--border-control` (measured 3:1 or better against the sheet and ground in WebKit). Notices in the feed (a failed run, an unconfirmed stop, a log that may be incomplete) are hairline-bounded on no fill, and feed disclosure rows hover like board rows. `RawLogView` needed no change: it was already tokens-only monospace at 11px with a muted line-number gutter. `FeedRow` and `RawLogView` render agent text through React's text path, and a finish test fails if a raw-HTML path appears in any of the five files.
