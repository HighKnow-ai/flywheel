## What the plan asked for, and what happened

**No brand-filled buttons (binding decision 1).** The register named `MergeConfirmSheet.tsx:377`. The same defect was in three more places in this group that the register did not list: the header's **Merge** in `ReviewSurface.tsx` and both **Open request** buttons in `EpicRequestSheet.tsx` (the bar's and the sheet's). All four had ink on `--accent-nontext` (3.05:1 light, 4.42:1 dark). They now carry `control-edge review-action`: the 3:1 `--border-control` edge, a transparent ground, ink text, weight 600 so the primary is still set apart from a plain Cancel. No `--text-on-accent` anywhere. WebKit measures each: ground transparent, edge at least 3:1, text at least 4.5:1, in both schemes.

**Destructive fills (binding decision 1, second half).** The group has none. The only destructive treatment is the conflict text in the merge sheet ("Merge aborted", "Will conflict in N files"). It was bare destructive-coloured text; it is now `.review-warn`, the destructive TEXT role (4.5:1 asserted per mode in `contrast.test.ts`) beside a `TriangleAlert` glyph, so it never rests on hue, and the plan's icon-pairing obligation holds. Asserted: no fill, a glyph, the role colour.

**Focus-ring defect class (binding decision 2).** Checked: none of the review components set an inline `outline`, and nothing in them is a prop-driven ring. The criteria rows (inside `AcceptanceCriteriaList`) were fixed in 08.1-15 and are covered by `finish.test.tsx`'s "no inline outline" check here; the changed-file rows and diff rows had no ring problem. The one related defect found in the group was different: **App.css washes every hovered button with `--divider`**, so a pointer resting on the just-clicked changed-file row erased its selected shift. `.review-file` and the pressed scope toggle re-assert their surface under hover. Logged for the pass that owns `App.css`.

**Overlay rule (binding decision 3).** `MergeConfirmSheet` (scrim 244, shadow 259) and `EpicRequestSheet` (scrim 287, shadow 302) used `rgba(0,0,0,0.45)` and `0 16px 48px rgba(0,0,0,0.5)`: a dark-tuned scrim that washes out on white, on the confirmation for an irreversible action. Both now use `.review-sheet-scrim` (`--scrim`) over `.review-sheet` (`--shadow-sheet`, and a `--border-control` edge, because a dark panel on a dark scrim has no other edge at 1.16:1). WebKit resolves the scrim to `rgba(31, 35, 40, 0.4)` light and `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)` dark, and the shadow to the role values.

**The diff tint (binding decision 4).** Not changed. The 3% tint from 08.1-07 was left alone, and the existing legibility test already measured only the roles the fixture happened to contain. I replaced that reliance with a new test that **reads every `.token.<role>` out of `review.css`** and measures each on added, removed and unchanged rows, on the selected variant of each, and the gutter text on its 22% tint, in both schemes. Result: every one at least 4.5:1. Weakest paints (from a run with the floor raised to print them): light 4.53:1, dark 4.51:1, both on a **selected** row (`blocked` and `brand` text roles on `--row-surface-selected`, the AA ceiling documented in `tokens.css`); unselected rows are higher. Gutter text is 11.0 to 16.9:1.

**That measurement found a real defect.** A selected diff line (the gutter click that anchors evidence) painted `react-diff-view`'s default `--diff-code-selected-background-color: #fffce0`, a pale yellow no token owns. Under light ink it passed; in **dark** it put every syntax role at 2.4 to 4.1:1 and the gutter number at 1.13:1. The selected line now reads as a board row's selection does (A-06): `--row-surface-selected` plus the 2px `--selection-rule` as an inset gutter shadow, so it is square and does not reflow. The text-selection wash (`--diff-selection-background-color`) had been set to `--row-surface-selected`, which is invisible as a text selection; it is now a neutral 18% ink wash.

**Motion (binding decision 5).** Untouched. The motion browser arm passes.

**Real data (binding decision 6).** The capture mounts the real `ReviewSurface` over THRE-58's three seeded criteria (from `comp-data.json`, the 08.1-03 rows, unchecked, as they are on the real board) and the real uncommitted `README.md` diff of its worktree. THRE-58 has no commits ahead today, so the second file is the comp's FIXTURE: a real commit of this repository (`CriteriaRail.tsx`, +49 -6), labelled on the page. The conflicted precheck for the destructive capture is also a labelled FIXTURE. Other seeded tasks (THRE-33, 38, 43, 53) were not captured: the surface renders the same rail for any task, and the fixture's one task is enough to show the three-row rail beside a long diff.
