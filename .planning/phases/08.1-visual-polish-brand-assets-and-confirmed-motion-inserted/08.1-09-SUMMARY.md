## The two questions, verbatim

The prior executor halted at Task 1 having written nothing; the owner answered both gates and a third question on 2026-10-08.

### Question 1 (A-05): "Settle the change cue's glyphs, and whether the mark registry's outline-only rule stays as written."

Context given: D-15 writes the cue as a triangle-with-count and a filled dot, but the registry's own rule is that a glyph is a named export from the icon package, outline-only, with no unicode character standing in for an icon system, and the legend test resolves every registered glyph against that package. Plan 01 therefore registered two real outline glyphs (`GitCommitVertical`, `Circle`).

Options offered, in full:

| id | Name | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| `keep` | Keep the two outline glyphs plan 01 registered | No contract change; the legend test already resolves them; the cue is live today | Not literally the triangle-and-dot D-15 sketched |
| `replace` | Use two different outline glyphs, named by the owner | Closer to the intended reading while keeping the registry rule intact | Plan 01's registry entries and the legend test counts change with it |
| `amend-rule` | Amend the registry rule to permit a filled glyph for data-channel marks | Delivers the literal triangle-and-dot reading | Loosens a published contract for every future mark, not just these two |

Resume signal: `Reply "keep", "replace" with two glyph names, or "amend the rule"`.

**Answer: `replace`, with `Triangle` and `Dot`.** `amend-rule` was explicitly declined.

### Question 2 (A-06): "Choose the row selection treatment, given what the mechanical craft detector actually said about the comped row."

Context given: D-12 specifies a 2px left rule plus a background shift, deliberately neutral. The craft floor bans a coloured side border above one pixel on a list item. All four row states must stay simultaneously distinguishable, and a selected-but-unfocused row must not look identical to a focused one.

Options offered, in full:

| id | Name | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| `rule-2px` | The 2px neutral rule as D-12 specifies | Exactly what the direction contract states; strongest read of "you are here" | May trip the craft floor's side-border clause, which would make this a standing exception |
| `rule-1px` | A one-pixel rule plus the background shift | Inside the craft floor with no exception needed | A weaker cursor against a hairline-heavy world where 1px rules are already everywhere |
| `inset-bar` | An inset bar drawn as a pseudo-element rather than a border | Keeps the 2px weight without being a border, so the clause does not apply | One more drawing mechanism on the hottest row in the product |
| `background-only` | Background shift only, no rule | Quietest option; nothing to flag | Must still keep all four row states distinguishable using background alone, the hardest version of that obligation |

Resume signal: `Reply "2px rule", "1px rule", "inset bar", or "background only"`.

**Answer: `2px rule`**, plus the row-surface shift, exactly D-12. The owner accepts the reading that `skill-ban-side-stripe-borders` targets accent colours rather than neutral ink.

**What the detector actually said (plan 04, full parse mode): 135 findings, all `warning`, zero errors** (115 `cramped-padding`, 14 `tiny-text`, 5 `undersized-ui-text`, 1 `flat-type-hierarchy`; `comps/detector-report.json`). It said nothing about selection, because the side-border ban is prose in `craft-floor.md` and not a `detect.mjs` antipattern. A-06 states that this silence is not approval, and does not record the detector as having cleared the rule.

### Question 3 (raised by the executor, decided by the owner): the focus state

The executor found `selected` and `selected-unfocused` effectively invisible. The orchestrator recomputed the ratios independently and I reproduced them a third time from `tokens.css`:

| Pair | Light | Dark |
|---|---|---|
| idle vs selected | 1.065 | 1.033 |
| selected vs selected-unfocused | **1.011** | **1.014** |
| hover vs selected | 1.028 | 1.021 |

**Answer: fix it in this phase, do not defer it.** The rule is full ink when the list has focus and lighter when it has lost focus. Accessibility is the one thing this phase does not defer and the token layer is already open.

**Treatment specified (concrete values, in A-06(b) and `08.1-UI-SPEC.md`):**

| Role | Light | Dark |
|---|---|---|
| `--selection-rule` (focused) | `#1f2328` | `#ededed` |
| `--selection-rule-unfocused` | `#7a828c` | `#707070` |
| focused rule vs unfocused rule | 4.06 | 4.23 |
| unfocused rule vs every row surface (lowest) | 3.65 | 3.87 |

Both states stay 2px wide with a reserved transparent left border on every task row, so a state change never reflows. The difference is tone.

## Observations the owner should know

- **The shipped surface shift is much weaker than the comp's.** `comps/comp.css` paints hover as a 4% and selected as an 8% mix of text colour over the ground. The shipped roles (plan 07) are opaque and no darker than the surface role: idle to selected is 1.065 light and 1.033 dark. Plan 07 chose that on purpose, because a 9% ink overlay failed blocked/done/failed text at 4.1 to 4.3:1, and it noted that "the 2px selection rule carries the signal". That is now true in a stronger sense than anyone planned: the rule is the main selection cue, and the finish reviewer should compare against the comp knowing the surface shift is thinner.
- **The commits-ahead count is not brand-coloured in the shipped cue.** `DIRECTION.md` § 2 makes it one of five coloured data channels; plan 01's cue paints it `--text-muted-strip`. The UI-SPEC records the DIRECTION as the intent and hands the reconciliation to plan 14.
- **`Dot` is also the `project-current` glyph** in the left rail. Different surface and channel, never in the same row as the change cue, so it does not recreate the `Circle` collision. Noted in A-05.
- **`lucide-react`'s `Dot` is small.** It was chosen by the owner and verified to exist. Whether it reads at 12px beside a status glyph is a perceptual question and is queued, not asserted.
