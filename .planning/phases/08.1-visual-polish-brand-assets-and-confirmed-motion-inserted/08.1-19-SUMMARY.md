## What the plan asked for, and what happened

**No fills (binding decision 1).** The two destructive fills in this group were `DeleteConfirm`'s Delete and `OrphanRecoveryModal`'s Kill All, each ink on `--destructive-strip` at 2.95:1 light and 2.86:1 dark, each with a stale comment claiming 6.3:1. Both are now `control-edge confirm-action confirm-action-destructive`: transparent ground, `--destructive-text-rack` for text and a 2px edge, an icon (`Trash2`, `OctagonX`) beside the word. WebKit asserts the ground is transparent, the text is 4.5:1 and the edge 3:1 on `--surface-secondary` in both schemes, that the icon takes the destructive colour, and that a lead and its 1px neighbour are the same height. The first-run and unreachable actions are the one 2px ink lead of their surface; no brand fill, no `--text-on-accent`. `--text-on-accent` is not referenced anywhere in the group.

**The focus-ring-on-prop defect class (binding decision 2).** Checked every focusable element in the group: none sets `outline` from a prop. `DoneWarning`'s `outline: "none"` on a non-interactive, programmatically focused container is the one exception, excepted by name as 08.1-18 did. `states-finish.test.tsx` fails if an `outline` appears in any other file of the group. The orphan modal's rows are list items with buttons, so their ring is the buttons' own `:focus-visible` (the brand keyboard ring, use 3 of the brand rule). The unreachable and first-run states have one button each and no row list.

**Overlay tier (binding decision 3).** The two scrims were `rgba(0,0,0,0.45)` and the two shadows `0 12px 32px rgba(0,0,0,0.45)` inline, with the divider as the edge. Both are `.confirm-scrim` (`--scrim`) over `.confirm-dialog` (`--shadow-dialog`, `--border-dialog`, `--radius-chrome`), the orphan modal wider at 560px. WebKit measures the computed scrim (`rgba(31, 35, 40, 0.4)` light, `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)` dark), opacity 1 and animation `none` on frame one, the edge (`#7a828c` light, `#909090` dark) and the shadow colour. It also asserts the real board's text reads 4.5:1 *beneath* the scrim in both schemes, so the scrim dims the board and does not erase it.

**Empty and error states against the real states (binding decision 4).** What is real and what is not:
- **Unreachable is the real second registration**, and rendering it taught something true: read off a copy of the fleet database, it is a **duplicate**. Same name (`threadfin`), same key prefix (`THRE`) and the very same `repo_path` as the active project. The contract copy ("expected threadfin at {path}, but the repository marker there no longer matches") is accurate about what Flywheel found, and "Locate Project…" would open a picker to be pointed at a folder the active project already owns. The copy is fixed by the Copywriting Contract and is unchanged. Recorded in `deferred-items.md` for the owner, with how a second registration of one path was ever accepted.
- **First run is not reachable in the real app** (two projects are registered), so it is rendered in isolation with no fixtures: it has no data.
- **The recovery modal's three runs are FIXTURES**, each labelled in its title, because the real board has no live orphan. Behind both confirmations is the real board: the project rail (with its own "Unreachable" entry), all 13 epic headers and the first epic's real tasks.
- The tray and triage lane's empty states and the "No epic" bucket belong to groups five and one and were not touched here.

**The text-input leftover (binding decision 5).** Closed for this group, and said so: `FirstRun`'s confirm-the-key field is `control-edge state-field` with a 2px ink `:focus-visible` ring, asserted at source and measured in WebKit (ink, not brand). `App.css` is not edited; the global `input:focus-visible` is still the brand for `QuickAdd`, `FilterBar`, the criteria composer and the title field, logged in `deferred-items.md`.

**The warning about dead styles.** The recovery modal still applies `.agent-pulse`, the class plan 13 deleted and plan 17 restored. The browser test asserts the glyph's computed animation is `agent-pulse` in the default arm and that under the reduced-motion arm the class is absent and opacity is 1. `states-and-confirms.css` itself has no `@keyframes`, `animation` or `transition`, asserted from the shipped stylesheet text.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 2 - Missing critical] The unreachable state had a glyph but not the word**
- **Found during:** Task 1
- **Issue:** the plan and the contract say the warning pairs an icon with the literal word, "an inherited never-colour-alone requirement". The component rendered a triangle in the destructive colour above a heading and had no such word; only the rail entry did.
- **Fix:** a row above the heading: the glyph (decorative) and the word "Unreachable", both in the destructive text role (asserted 4.5:1). The word is the rail's own. No Copywriting Contract string changed.
- **Verification:** `states-finish.test.tsx` and the WebKit test assert glyph and word.
- **Committed in:** `c5d162a`

**2. [Rule 2 - Missing critical] Delete had no icon, so removing the fill would have left it distinguishable by hue alone**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** the plan requires every destructive treatment to be icon-paired. `Delete` was a bare word on a red fill; once the fill goes (AA), nothing but hue would tell it from an ordinary action.
- **Fix:** `Trash2` beside Delete and `OctagonX` beside Kill All, inside the button, `aria-hidden` so the accessible name is unchanged.
- **Committed in:** `b24d538`

**3. [Rule 3 - Blocking, in the test] A Tab-trap probe cannot run in WebKit**
- **Found during:** Task 3
- **Issue:** the first draft proved the modal traps focus by pressing Tab. WebKit does not Tab to buttons by default (documented in `review/__tests__/browser/keyboard.browser.test.tsx`), so focus never moved and the assertion failed for a reason that said nothing about the product.
- **Fix:** replaced by a hit test: the element at a real board row's centre is inside the blocking layer, plus Escape and a scrim click both leaving the dialog mounted. The focus trap itself stays pinned in jsdom (`orphan-recovery.test.tsx`), where Tab-free focus assertions are valid.
- **Committed in:** `5aa286d`

**4. [Rule 2 - Spec kept true] `DoneWarning` was not changed**
- **Issue:** the plan lists it; plan 18 had already done its tier. What was left (spacing, type) was already on the locked scale, and moving it onto classes would have meant rewriting plan 18's source-level assertions for no visible change. It is captured and its tier is re-measured in WebKit here (popover edge, no scrim, no animation on frame one).

**5. [Rule 2 - Scale] The orphan footnote moved from 10px micro to the locked 11px label**
- The footnote warns that the listed processes are spending tokens right now. It was the smallest text in a blocking dialog, and the detector flags `tiny-text`. One usage moved; the role's fate stays the owner's open call.

**6. A hairline between the orphan runs**
- Added so each run reads as its own in a list that is the point of the dialog. The last run has none; asserted.
