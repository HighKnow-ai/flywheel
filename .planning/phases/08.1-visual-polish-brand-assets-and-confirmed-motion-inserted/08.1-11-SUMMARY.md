# Phase 08.1 Plan 11: The Epic Roster Summary

**Epic header rows that read their progress, status distribution, agents and waiting proposals from the board-wide aggregate (printing "no criteria yet" instead of a zero), a change signal rolled up from the single change-flags map, headers that are keyboard-selectable by epic id, and a pure computed summary line above the roster.**

## Accomplishments

- `EpicHeaderRow` renders title, key sub-label, progress bar plus printed `N of M`, criteria as `K of T criteria` or `no criteria yet`, a labelled proportional distribution strip with a legend line, agents running and proposals waiting. The progress fill is `--accent-nontext`; the segments are neutral tones mixed from the text role, with `done` in the done role. No colour literal is declared.
- Every figure is the aggregate's. The fixture for the loaded-rows case holds 2 task rows under an epic whose aggregate says 12 of 60, and the header reads 12 of 60.
- Plan 10's open limit is closed: the header is a selection target keyed by the epic id. Click selects and toggles; `j`/`k` land on it; Enter opens detail; `r` continues shaping.
- The change signal is a reduction over the one map `useChangeFlags` already returns. Measurable commits are summed, dirty becomes one mark, an all-unmeasurable clean epic renders nothing, and the figure joins the row's accessible name ("5 commits ahead across its tasks").
- `summaryLine` is one pure function with no imports. The line renders above the rack in the epic lens once the aggregate has landed.

## Discretionary summary clauses

The four clauses CONTEXT names are mandatory: decisions pending, items reviewable, agents blocked and for how long, epics with no criteria. Two were added, each answered by an aggregate already fetched, with no new query:

- **Failing builds** ("N tasks have a failing build"), from `ciFailedTaskIds`, which `useForgeFlags` already supplies to the rack.
- **Agents running** ("N agents running"), from the sum of `agentsRunning` in the epic aggregate.

Sources of the four mandatory clauses: decisions pending is the pending-proposal count plus `recoveryTaskIds`; reviewable is the sum of `in_review` over the aggregate (including the no-epic bucket); blocked is `board_blocked_summary`'s count; no-criteria counts aggregate entries with a real epic id and `criteria: null`.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 2 - Missing critical] The change roll-up needed the epic id on each change flag**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** The plan reduces the change-flags map per epic and says the epic-to-task relationship is available in `BoardView`. It is not: the only task rows in memory are the 200-row window and whichever epics are expanded, so a roll-up over them silently drops worktrees. That is the confident-lie failure T-08.1-28 names. A "no change signal" next to an epic that has live work is false.
- **Fix:** Added `epic_id` (nullable) to `ChangeFlagInput`, `ChangeFlag` and `ChangeFlagDto`, selected from the `tasks` row the query already joins, and `epicId` to the TS `ChangeFlagDto`. No new query, table or migration.
- **Files modified:** `packages/core/src/query/change_flags.rs`, `packages/desktop/src-tauri/src/commands/change_flags.rs`, `packages/desktop/src/adapters/tauri.ts`, `packages/core/tests/change_flags.rs`
- **Verification:** `a_flag_names_the_epic_its_task_belongs_to_so_the_board_can_roll_it_up`; `cargo check` on the desktop crate; the six roll-up cases.
- **Committed in:** `636c03e`

**2. [Rule 2 - Missing critical] Detail panel showed an empty Status control for an epic of unknown status**

- **Fix:** `ItemDetailPanel` renders the Status control only when a status is known; `BoardView` passes `undefined` rather than an empty string.
- **Files modified:** `packages/desktop/src/item-detail/ItemDetailPanel.tsx` (not in the plan's file list), `packages/desktop/src/board/BoardView.tsx`
