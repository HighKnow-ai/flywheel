## Accomplishments

- `tokens.css` carries the light values in `:root` with `color-scheme: light dark`, and a `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` block that re-declares every mirrored name. All 34 legacy property names survive; five were added (`--border-control`, `--scrim`, `--shadow-popover`, `--shadow-dialog`, `--shadow-sheet`).
- `--border-control` is `#7a828c` light and `#707070` dark: 3.65 and 3.87 on the worse surface. The divider stays a hairline (1.16 to 1.43) and is asserted as decorative on purpose.
- The dark accent text lift was derived, not eyeballed: hue 225, saturation held at the brand's 70.3%, lightness stepped 1% from 55%. Sixty percent is the first step to clear 4.5. Result `#5175e1`, 4.55 on `#0f0f0f` and 4.70 on `#0a0a0a`. The retired lift `#7d99ff` was 7.19, over-lifted. Light mode has no lift: the brand is 4.86 on the surface already.
- `theme/tokens.ts` now exports `LIGHT`, `DARK`, `MODES`, `PALETTES` and `ALIASES`. `RACK`, `STRIP`, `INK` and `AGENT` are gone, so a role declared in one mode only no longer typechecks.
- The contrast test iterates `MODES`, parses `tokens.css` per colour-scheme block, and carries a negative case for every role. It was observed red before the rewrite (see TDD Gate Compliance).
- A real `BoardRow` mounted in WebKit clears its floors against the colour actually painted, in both schemes. The full browser arm is green in both: 26 tests under each config.

## Decisions Made

- **Row surfaces are opaque, no darker than the surface role.** The old selection overlays were brand-blue alpha (6% / 14%). The brand no longer marks selection (D-12), and a neutral 9% ink overlay failed blocked/done/failed text at 4.1-4.3:1. Opaque `#fafbfc / #f6f8fa / #f8f9fb` (light) and `#0c0c0c / #0f0f0f / #0d0d0d` (dark) keep every role AA on every row state. The cost is that the shifts are subtle; the 2px selection rule (plan 09's decision) carries the signal.
- **ProjectRail uses `--row-surface-selected`** instead of a new tint role. The old literal was byte-identical to the old role. A brand tint role had no AA room in dark with the lifted accent text (4.07 to 4.54 on a tint).
- **Strip roles are aliases, not removed.** Four files consume `--strip-*` and more consume the strip text roles; plan 10 rewrites the row.
