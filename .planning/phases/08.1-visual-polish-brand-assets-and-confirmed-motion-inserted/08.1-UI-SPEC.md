**Status: ADOPTED.** Adopted 2026-10-07 by the owner (Cyril Marechal), wholesale (D-03), after viewing
all six rendered comps (mission-control, item-detail, review, each in light and dark). Adoption voids

**The world.** Near-neutral ground, one surface family, one-pixel hairline rules, no fills. Type and

bar, value, change delta, with the task-distribution strip, the agents-running count and the
proposals-waiting count beside it. The status-band rack survives as a grouping lens over the same
board (A-02).

**Said plainly.** The comps are static HTML and CSS rendered through this project's own WebKit engine,
not image-generated, because no image generation exists in this environment. That is a disclosed
substitution (`08.1-RESEARCH.md` Correction 11). Values the real board could not supply (agent
challenge, running agent, commits-ahead) are labelled FIXTURE in the comps.

**The honesty rule (D-06).** Progress is tasks done over total. An epic whose tasks carry no criteria
prints `no criteria yet`, never a `0%` that lies.

### The control-boundary role, and why it exists

A hairline rule cannot be a control's border. WCAG 1.4.11 asks 3:1 for the visible edge of an input or
button, and `--divider` in dark is 1.42 on the ground, still a hairline. Without a second role, filter inputs, pickers and
quick-add lose their visible edge the moment the rule goes hairline. `--border-control` is that role.
Inputs, selects, chips, pickers and buttons draw their boundary with it. `--divider` separates regions
and rows only.

### The dark brand-text lift

The pinned brand `#3c64dd` is 3.83 and 3.71 in dark: enough for a fill (3:1 non-text), too little for
text. The lift was derived by search, not by eye: hue 225, saturation held at the brand's 70.3%,
lightness stepped 1% from 55%. The first step to clear 4.5 on the worst dark surface is 60%, giving
`#5175e1`. Light mode carries no lift. The old lifts `#7d99ff` and `#2d4fb8` are void.

### Selection: the 2px rule (A-06) and the focus clause (A-06(b))

This subsection is binding and carries the two amendment entries.

**The treatment (A-06(a)).** Selection on a task row is a 2px left border plus a surface shift. The
border is neutral ink, never a brand colour. This is a deliberate, reasoned judgement and not a
detector result: the craft floor's side-border ban (`craft-floor.md` line 35, "a *colored* `border-left`
or `border-right` above 1px on cards, list items, callouts, or alerts") is read as targeting accent
colours, and neutral ink marking the single current position is outside it. **The craft detector did not
clear this rule and could not.** The side-border ban is prose in the floor document, not a `detect.mjs`
antipattern, so the detector's silence on selection is structural, not approval. If the ban is ever read
to cover neutral ink, this rule stands as a **knowing exception recorded in A-06**, not an oversight.

**The focus clause (A-06(b)).** The two row surfaces for "selected" and "selected, list not focused" are
effectively the same colour: they are 1.011 apart in light and 1.014 apart in dark. The rule therefore
carries the distinction.

| State | Rule | Surface |

| idle | none | `--row-surface` |
| hover | none | `--row-surface-hover` |
| selected, list has focus | 2px `--selection-rule` | `--row-surface-selected` |
| selected, list has lost focus | 2px `--selection-rule-unfocused` | task row: `--row-surface` (idle); epic row: `--row-surface-selected-unfocused` |

## Overlay Rule

Plan 07 shipped per-mode scrim and shadow roles but left 14 components hard-coding a black scrim or
shadow, which is wrong on a white ground. Plans 14 through 19 close that. This rule is what they
implement against.

1. **No literal colour, scrim or shadow outside `styles/tokens.css` and `theme/tokens.ts`.** An overlay
   backdrop is `var(--scrim)`. An overlay's elevation is one of three `var(--shadow-*)` roles.
2. **Tier is by kind, not by taste.**
   - **Popover**: anchored to a trigger, dismissed by outside click, does not dim the page. Carries
     `--shadow-popover` and **no scrim**.
   - **Dialog**: a centred modal that asks for a decision. Carries `--scrim` and `--shadow-dialog`.
   - **Sheet**: a large modal workspace. Carries `--scrim` and `--shadow-sheet`.
3. **An overlay never interpolates opacity on mount** (see § Motion). The scrim is at full value on the
   first frame.
4. **Tier is told by the edge in dark.** A shadow over a near-black ground is invisible, so the three tiers
   are physically indistinguishable by shadow alone in dark (`rgba(0,0,0,0.6/0.65/0.7)`). Each tier draws its
   own edge role instead (`--border-popover`, `--border-dialog`, `--border-sheet`): equal to `--border-control`
   in light, where the shadow carries the tier, and a step lighter per tier in dark. The dark scrim is 0.4 so
   a modal still shows the board it sits over.
5. **A highlighted or chosen row's 2px rule sits inside the overlay's content box**, never on the overlay's own
   border, where it reads as a thickened dialog edge. The overlay pads its list by one gutter (8px).
6. **Radius follows kind**: dialogs, sheets, popovers and the palette use `--radius-chrome`.

## Radius Language

Derived from `comps/comp.css`, which the owner adopted wholesale. The comps draw three shapes and no
others.

| Shape | Radius | Token | Used by |
|-------|--------|-------|---------|

| Marks | 3px | `--radius-mark` (new, plan 14) | the progress track and fill, the task-distribution strip, small tags and inline boxes |

The focus ring follows the radius of whatever shape it wraps, as before: square on a row, 6px on a

| panel slide-in | ~220ms | A peer surface is arriving anchored to the list, not a modal takeover. It travels the furthest, so it earns the longest time. | **220ms**, confirmed unchanged | 2026-10-08 |

**Confirmed 2026-10-08 by the owner, who opened and watched `comps/motion.html` (all four motions,
once) before answering.** He confirmed all four unchanged. The numbers above are an observation, not an
assertion. Three things he was shown and accepted:

- **The easing is front-loaded**, so each label overstates the felt duration. 90% of travel is done at
  about 73ms (panel 220), 59ms (collapse 180), 46ms (palette 140) and 40ms (selection 120).
- **Two figures are the implementer's judgement, not derived:** the palette's 8px settle distance (plan
  09's cap, itself flagged as judgement) and reading "selection move" as the row's background wash.
- **Row reorder follows collapse at 180ms.** Row travel uses the collapse duration; the dead
  `--duration-displace` (220ms) token was retired the same day (see 08.1-13-SUMMARY.md).

Of the two durations that shipped before this phase, `--duration-displace` (220ms, never reviewed) is
now removed. `--duration-settle` (180ms, the colour wash on a settling control) remains; it is not one of
the four and was not separately shown to the owner, so it is still an unreviewed assertion.

### The palette and "never a fade": settled

The old contract contradicted itself. `01-UI-SPEC.md` § Motion said "the command palette fades/scales in
on open (standard palette pattern)", while its own motion grammar said "**Never a fade** — a strip moves
or it does not." D-20 voids both, so this section decides.

**Decision: the grammar wins. The palette does not fade and does not scale.**

- It arrives at **full opacity on the first frame**, and its scrim is at full value on the first frame.
- It settles into place over the palette-open duration with a **transform-only vertical translation** of
  no more than 8px. Plan 13 picks the distance and records it.
- It mounts only while open. There is no exit animation: it unmounts, and success criterion 3 does not
  ask for one.

Reasons: an opacity ramp on an overlay the user is already typing into makes the first frames
low-contrast text over a half-transparent scrim; a scale-in moves the focus target while it is being
aimed at; and a fade is the slowest way to deliver the "must feel instant" intent that the 140ms row
states.

**The one carve-out.** `02-UI-SPEC.md` § Motion Additions keeps its scoped exception for the running
session's `Loader` pulse (`agent-pulse` in `motion.css`), an opacity animation. It signals that a process
is alive; it does not announce a state change. "Never a fade" governs state changes and overlay mounts,
and nothing else.

### What moves, and why

- **The detail panel** slides in from the trailing edge. Transform, never width: board rows declare a
  container query type, so animating width would re-evaluate their container ladder every frame.
- **A group** collapsing or expanding settles its rows. The existing displacement utility moves the rows
  below; newly mounted rows get a short enter keyframe. Removed rows disappear immediately. No container
  height is animated, no grid row-template trick is used, nothing is measured.
- **The command palette** opens as decided above.
- **The selection** moving between rows transitions the surface and the rule, signalling "you moved".

### What must never move

- Unrelated rows on a pushed-delta refetch. One row's edit must not visually jostle its neighbours.
- Anything on page load. There is no page-load choreography.
- Hover. No bounce, no icon wiggle, no decorative motion beyond a static colour change.
- The activity trail. It renders fully populated, instantly, on open.
- State-bearing surfaces. The orphan-recovery modal, the missing-worktree banner, the abandoned-run
  acknowledgment, the Needs Decision pill, the destructive-gate card, the permission-request card, the
  git lock-retry row and a worktree-removal dialog's Checking-to-Clean swap all appear, change and
  disappear as plain state changes: no entrance animation, no cross-fade. This restates the substance of
  the voided `03-UI-SPEC.md` § Motion Additions as one rule.
- Numerals. A cost or token figure updates with no odometer.

### `prefers-reduced-motion`

**Hard requirement, inherited unchanged.** When `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` is set, every motion in
this section collapses to an instant state change (snap, no interpolation). The state change itself is
never removed. `styles/motion.css` already zeroes the duration custom properties and applies a blanket
`animation-duration: 0s !important; transition-duration: 0s !important` rule under that media query. New
motion must stay in CSS so that rule keeps catching it, and must not use `requestAnimationFrame` or
`.animate()`. The running-session pulse is omitted entirely under reduced motion rather than frozen
mid-cycle.

## Row Anatomy Additions

Row Anatomy's field semantics in `01-UI-SPEC.md` stay authoritative. Two amendments add to them.

**The change-cue slot (A-03, glyphs per A-05).** A graded annotation lives beside the status glyph, in
the agent-signal channel next to the CI-failure and proposal-pending signals. It is information, not an
alert: not a button, not a pill with a background, and not colour-only.

| Cue | Glyph | Reads as |
|-----|-------|----------|
| commits ahead | `Triangle` plus the count | work waiting to be reviewed |

A task with no worktree renders nothing. Zero ahead and "cannot measure" both render nothing. Both glyphs

glyphs were declined). On an epic row the cue cannot use the slot that holds the pending-proposal count

### The Performance Contract survives

`01-UI-SPEC.md` § Performance Contract is restated as it stands, with the one change A-03 examined. The
