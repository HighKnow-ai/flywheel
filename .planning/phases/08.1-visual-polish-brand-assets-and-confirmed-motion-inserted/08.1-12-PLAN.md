<objective>
Surface the work Phases 5 and 6 already shipped. Agents raise challenges against epics today, and the
owner only sees them as a small count behind a tray and a triage lane. D-08 moves the read into the
place the decision is actually made.

Implements **D-08** (an agent's challenge renders inline, above the epic's tasks, because answering a
challenge may change what the tasks are; accepting one creates a task in place, so the roadmap grows
itself) and completes the expand half of **D-05** (expanding an epic reveals its content in place).

**What this plan does not do.** It does not delete, replace or deprecate the proposal tray or the
triage lane. D-08 says they keep working, and the triage lifecycle is Phase 6's own contract. This is
a second read surface over the same data and the same mutations — not a second implementation of
either.

This plan builds only what amendment A-01 approved, since an inline challenge row only exists inside
an expanded epic. Read `08.1-AMENDMENTS.md` first.

Probe fallback: skipped — this phase has no REQ-IDs to probe (visible skip). `must_haves` derive
from SC0–SC4, the CONTEXT decisions and `08.1-VALIDATION.md`'s requirement-to-test map.

Wire the accept and reject affordances to the **existing** mutations. Pass only the proposal
identifier; the mutation and the core own everything else. Do not add a second accept path, a second

lifecycle, its events and its triage consequences are Phase 5 and 6's contract. A second path is how

board-changed invalidation rather than invalidating by hand at the call site.

error: this is the surface where a decision is made, and a silently vanished challenge is worse than
a visible failure. The sixth `<behavior>` case asserts that.
