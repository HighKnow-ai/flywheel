## Phase goal

**Goal (ROADMAP, verbatim):** Every structural guarantee the milestone is about to lean on is
closed-world and asserted *before* the first new record type exists — the spine cannot erode, an
unclassified table fails the build, authority is reachable only from a human, the context packet is
bounded, and the fields nobody reads are gone rather than drafted.

> MVP note: the ROADMAP Goal line is not in user-story form and no story was invented. See 13-01.

<objective>
F9: surfaces are chosen today by an eight-deep nested ternary in `App.tsx`, outside the keyboard
layer model that governs everything else about which layer is on top. Phases 14-18 add at least
three more surfaces (Cycle, PRD, ADR). Make the surface one modelled value with one resolver inside
`state/keyboard.ts`, and make adding a surface outside the model a TypeScript build failure.

Purpose: criterion 5's first half, and the structural precondition for the PRD and ADR surfaces that
land in Phase 16 — "one surface union before the PRD/ADR surfaces", in the research round's words.
Output: a `UiSurface` union, an `activeSurface` resolver, an exhaustive `switch` in `App.tsx`, and a
test pinning the precedence order.
</objective>
