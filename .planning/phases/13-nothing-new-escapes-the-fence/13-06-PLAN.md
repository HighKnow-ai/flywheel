## Phase goal

**Goal (ROADMAP, verbatim):** Every structural guarantee the milestone is about to lean on is
closed-world and asserted *before* the first new record type exists — the spine cannot erode, an
unclassified table fails the build, authority is reachable only from a human, the context packet is
bounded, and the fields nobody reads are gone rather than drafted.

> MVP note: the ROADMAP Goal line is not in user-story form and no story was invented. See 13-01.

<objective>
Give the `backlog` band one meaning and make priority opt-in. Hand-made tasks are created at `todo`
(D-18) — typing a task is the human act, so `backlog` comes to mean exactly "proposed by an agent,
not yet accepted". Priority keeps its column and its CHECK (D-16); what changes is the writer: an
agent proposes a priority through the existing staging path carrying its reasoning, and a human
accepts it per item inside the acceptance transaction, which is why F4's `set_priority_in_tx`
(built in plan 13-03) is now required rather than optional (D-17).

Purpose: criterion 3's "priority is reachable only as an explicit opt-in suggestion carrying its
reasoning", and the band semantics the derived-status work in plan 13-07 builds on.
Output: a one-meaning `backlog` band, a priority proposal field, a two-sided gate, and the human's
control untouched.
</objective>
