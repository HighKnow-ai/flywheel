<objective>
Close criterion 4's one FAILED clause — "No unbounded read of summaries, decisions or records
remains" (FNC-05) — at all three sites the verifier named, and remove the blind spot that let two of
them ship.

Purpose: `decision_ids_on_record` runs on every preview and every launch, costs
`candidates × summaries × decisions`, and its caller turns any error into "no id on record" —

a false `drift.detected`. That is the exact false positive the function was added to remove.
Output: one bounded statement, a handled error path, a bounded epic-packet read, and `drift.rs`
inside the gate.
</objective>
