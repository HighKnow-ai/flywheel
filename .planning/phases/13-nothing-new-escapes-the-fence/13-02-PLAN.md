## Phase goal

**Goal (ROADMAP, verbatim):** Every structural guarantee the milestone is about to lean on is
closed-world and asserted *before* the first new record type exists — the spine cannot erode, an
unclassified table fails the build, authority is reachable only from a human, the context packet is
bounded, and the fields nobody reads are gone rather than drafted.

> MVP note: the ROADMAP Goal line is not in user-story form and no story was invented. See 13-01.

<objective>
Bound the context packet at its input, not its fold (D-01). `SUMMARIES_SQL` gains a fixed `LIMIT`
read from one new const; `compact_summaries` is untouched and stays lossless over whatever list it
is handed; the boundary sentence tells the reader what was left out and how to reach it; and
`drift.rs`'s decision read-back detector changes **in this same plan** so the LIMIT never
manufactures a false `FabricatedDecisionEcho` (D-05, rated `costly` for exactly that reason).

Purpose: `/clear` stays free as task histories get long. This is the scale ceiling the research
round named as the single biggest threat to the product thesis.
Output: a bounded packet, a visible boundary, a scale test over a named fixture, and the
unbounded-reads gate widened to the file that was previously exempt.
</objective>
