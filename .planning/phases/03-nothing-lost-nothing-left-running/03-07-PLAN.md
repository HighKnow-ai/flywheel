<objective>
Make AGNT-07's word — *always* — true, given that Phase 2 proved a single mechanism cannot deliver
it (D-55, D-56, D-57, D-58).

**Phase goal (quoted verbatim from ROADMAP.md):** "Cyril can quit, crash or interrupt Flywheel at
any moment and know that no uncommitted work was destroyed and no agent is still spending his
money."

Purpose: success criterion 5 — "An agent running in auto-accept permission mode still stops and asks
before a force-push, a branch deletion or a recursive delete." Two findings force the shape of this
plan and neither may be planned around:

1. **Phase 2's 02-05 spike** found the installed backend applies its own built-in bash-command
   safety classification *before* ever consulting a configured `--permission-prompt-tool`. So the
   Phase 2 permission gate alone cannot satisfy a requirement whose word is *always*.
2. **03-RESEARCH.md reproduced live, twice, against the installed binary** that a `PreToolUse` hook
   which does not resolve within its own `timeout` is cancelled *and the tool call it was supposed
   to block executes anyway* under an auto-accept permission mode. So **the shim, not the hook, must
   be the component that blocks indefinitely.** The hook's job is narrower: fast, synchronous
   classification, and covering what the shim structurally cannot see.

Output: one compiled classifier, the shim's blocking gate over both `git` and `rm`, the injected
hook, and the `destructive_category` column plan 03-12's card variant reads.
</objective>
