**User story:** **As a** developer who has now seen five rounds of the same bypass class, **I want**
the corpus to manufacture its own obfuscated variants from the rows it already has, **so that** the
next narrowing of the scanner fails a test instead of shipping.

Purpose: `03-REVIEW.md`'s WR-02 names the reason round five shipped undetected — Group 3 of
`LAUNCHER_CORPUS` exercises every shadow keyword, and every row spells it literally. The corpus
cannot catch the class it was written for. Appending two hand-written obfuscated rows would fix the
one shape that was found and leave the next one uncovered, which is the pattern
`03-DESTRUCTIVE-GATE-BYPASS-CLASS.md` identifies as the actual finding. The fix is to state the
property instead: obfuscating a word the gate keys on must not change the gate's decision, asserted
over every row the corpus holds, now and in future.
