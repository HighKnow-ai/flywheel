## Phase goal

**Goal (ROADMAP, verbatim):** Cyril sees where the work stands without going to look — the current
Cycle's goal and progress and the roadmap ahead of it, folded from real counts — and the board
finally carries saturated state colour on a surface worth looking at, with colour as the fastest
channel and never the only one.

> MVP note: the ROADMAP `**Goal:**` line for Phase 14 is not in user-story form and no story was
> invented. See 14-01.

<objective>
The surface worth looking at. Every visual decision in this phase is judged on a board carrying the
real work at realistic density, never on fixture rows — that is the diagnosis the v0.1.0 milestone
audit produced and the reason criterion 5 is written the way it is.

Seeded from Flywheel's own `.planning/`: this milestone, these phases, these plans, these pending
todos. The lead row is real work because it IS the work, and judging the surface and using the
product become the same act. The runtime states that planning files cannot carry are synthesised on
top, deterministically, so every state class is guaranteed present and the board does not change
under the owner while he judges a visual direction on it.

Purpose: VIS-06 and criterion 5's demonstration board.
Output: one dev-only seeder binary, one launch script, and a test asserting the board it produces
actually carries every state at a named floor.
</objective>
