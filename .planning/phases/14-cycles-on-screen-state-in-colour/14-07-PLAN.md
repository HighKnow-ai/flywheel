## Phase goal

**Goal (ROADMAP, verbatim):** Cyril sees where the work stands without going to look — the current
Cycle's goal and progress and the roadmap ahead of it, folded from real counts — and the board
finally carries saturated state colour on a surface worth looking at, with colour as the fastest
channel and never the only one.

> MVP note: the ROADMAP `**Goal:**` line for Phase 14 is not in user-story form and no story was
> invented. See 14-01.

<objective>
One vocabulary, one consumer, one recorded decision.

Criterion 4 is an atomicity requirement, not a documentation chore: the decision, the specification,
the brand allowlist and the tests that enforce them move together or the change is not done. So this
plan writes `14-DIRECTION.md` with its SUPERSEDES table naming D-11, amends the specification section
`brand-rule.test.ts` reads as bytes, moves the allowlist, and deletes `RUN_STATUS_COLOR_ROLES` with
all five of its consumers rewired through the component plan 14-04 built — in one change.

The hard part is the allowlist's equality: it fails in both directions, so every brand use this
rewire removes must retire from `BRAND_USES` and every `USE` phrase it orphans must be struck from
the specification. That bookkeeping is the mechanism that makes criterion 4 real.

Purpose: VIS-02 and criterion 4 in full, and the deletion half of VIS-01.
Output: one direction document, one amended specification section, one moved allowlist, one deleted
export, and five rewired consumers.
</objective>
