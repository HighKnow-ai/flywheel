**Goal (ROADMAP, verbatim):** Cyril sees where the work stands without going to look — the current
Cycle's goal and progress and the roadmap ahead of it, folded from real counts — and the board
finally carries saturated state colour on a surface worth looking at, with colour as the fastest
channel and never the only one.

> invented. See 14-01.

<objective>
The two halves of RECD-10, both derived rather than stored.

Closing a Cycle becomes a per-epic question with three answers and no shortcut — no apply-to-all,
no pre-selected default — because the item is the unit of acceptance and a default on every row is a
bulk accept in disguise. And a Cycle whose scope changed after it started says the true sentence:
*"2 epics added, 1 removed since this Cycle started"*, read from the append-only event log, which
catches a swap that a stored baseline count never could and adds no column to the allowlist plan
14-01 pinned.

Purpose: RECD-10 in full and the second half of criterion 2.
Output: one close writer with a human-only gate and a refusal the IPC path cannot bypass, one
derived-drift query, one dialog, and two scoped test targets.
</objective>
