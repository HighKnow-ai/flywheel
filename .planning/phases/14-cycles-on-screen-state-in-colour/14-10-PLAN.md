## Phase goal

**Goal (ROADMAP, verbatim):** Cyril sees where the work stands without going to look — the current
Cycle's goal and progress and the roadmap ahead of it, folded from real counts — and the board
finally carries saturated state colour on a surface worth looking at, with colour as the fastest
channel and never the only one.

> MVP note: the ROADMAP `**Goal:**` line for Phase 14 is not in user-story form and no story was
> invented. See 14-01.

<objective>
The last gate, and the one the whole phase is built to survive: the owner looks at the running
application on the demonstration board and says yes or no, with a screenshot recorded.

Criterion 6 is specific about what does not count. A comp does not count. The direction document does
not count. A browser-harness render of the component does not count. And it cannot be moved to a
later phase — which is why this plan sits at the end of this one rather than as a line in Phase 18's
scope.

Everything except the judgement is automated, which is the shape to reach for whenever a criterion
needs a human: the script builds, stands the application up on the demo database, captures the window
at OS level in both appearances, and writes a manifest saying exactly which binary and which database
produced each image. What is left is the owner's yes or no and his words.

Purpose: VIS-07 and criterion 6.
Output: one capture script, the committed images, one committed verdict.
</objective>

## Edge coverage (spec-less probe fallback)

Phase-wide arithmetic: **24 probe-surfaced edges == 22 authored into `must_haves.truths` + 2
surfaced as flagged assumptions.** This plan authors 3 of the 22 and carries none of the 2 flagged.

**VIS-07:**

- **empty** (`verification: explicit`) — an absent or empty verdict file, or a verdict with no words
  of the owner's own, is not a sign-off; the phase cannot close on a missing file, and the file's
  existence, its explicit yes or no and its quoted words are each a verify command.
- **adjacency** (`verification: backstop`) — a screenshot that exists but records a comp or a harness
  render rather than the running application does not satisfy the criterion. The manifest and the
  ground-pixel sanity check make this strongly evidenced, but "this is the running application" is
  finally a reading of the image, so it abstains to `human_needed` rather than claiming a mechanical
  proof.
- **ordering** (`verification: backstop`) — the verdict is recorded after the capture, and a negative
  verdict loops back into this phase's tuning rather than forward. The first half is evidenced by the
  file's own reference to the images; the second can only be confirmed by what happens after a no is
  recorded, so it abstains.

## Prohibitions (spec-less probe fallback)

Phase-wide arithmetic: **3 kept prohibitions == 3 authored into `must_haves.prohibitions` + 0
surfaced as flagged assumptions.** This plan authors none; they are carried by 14-02, 14-04 and
14-08.

The adversarial candidate this plan's work raised — *the verdict becomes a rubber stamp, a screenshot
captured and a yes typed without looking* — was considered and DROPPED rather than minted, because the
mechanism that would defeat a rubber stamp is already in the criteria themselves and in this plan's
structure: the owner looks at the running application rather than the image, the verdict must quote
his own words rather than carry a bare yes, and plan 14-09's recorded outstanding items must be shown
to him by name. Minting a prohibition for it would restate those requirements rather than add a
constraint.
