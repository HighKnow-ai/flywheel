<objective>
Make the round-eight bypass mechanically catchable, land the corpus evidence plan 03-35's boundary
amendment earned, price the friction, and leave the next verifier a written coverage map — without
touching a requirement status.

This carries the third and fourth of `03-VERIFICATION.md`'s four `missing:` items. The third is the
one that matters most for the next round: `OBFUSCATION_SPLIT_KINDS` already sweeps
empty-double-quote, empty-single-quote and backslash splits over eleven keyed words and every corpus
row — `env` and `command` included — and it caught round six's backslash-split `env` defect before a
human had to. It has no brace-expansion kind, which is precisely why round eight shipped with a human
reading `main.rs` as the only detector. Adding the fourth kind means the CR-01 payload
`{env,} -S "-P /tmp git push --force"` is GENERATED from the row `env -S "-P /tmp git push --force"`
rather than hand-written, and so is every sibling nobody has thought of yet.

Purpose: this plan exists separately from 03-35, after it, specifically so the
document-before-corpus ordering is a real dependency in the plan graph rather than a comment inside
one plan — the same reason 03-33 and 03-34 were split. The trust-boundary document's own rule for
round six is that a newly found bypass shape is a boundary failure: the document is amended first,
and only then does a row belong in `LAUNCHER_CORPUS`. In D-62's terms this plan carries item 1 (the
checked-in adversarial corpus, asserted through the real hook against the real shim rather than
against the classifier in isolation) while plan 03-35 carried item 3 (the recorded trust boundary);
D-62 item 2's live `claude` run stands from plan 03-27 and is not re-run here.

Output: a generator that would have caught round eight; a corpus that carries the round-eight shapes
and prices their cost; a structural gate that keeps the C5/C6 clauses findable; and a bookkeeping
record mapping the four `missing:` items to the two plans that covered them.

**Tracer note.** This phase's tracer slice is plan 03-35 Task 1 (the boundary claim, C6's
implementation, and the `{env,}` shape proven end-to-end through the real compiled hook). Under
`TRACER_MODE` one shape leads one phase, so the three tasks here are EXPANSION tasks building out
from that proven slice — not a second tracer. They are still vertical: each takes a shape or a class
from unproven to asserted through the real hook against the real shim, never a layer in isolation.

Three assumptions specific to this plan:

1. **The brace split kind is generated only for complete-shell-word occurrences, and that is a
   narrowing with a measured justification rather than a convenience.** A brace group wrapping a
   complete word resolves back to exactly that word; a brace group glued to a suffix resolves to two
   words and a different command entirely. Generating the second kind and asserting decision
   invariance for it would assert something the shell does not do. The guard is asserted from both
   sides: `braceVariantCount` has a floor (so it cannot match nothing) and the `PATH=` row is named
   in the acceptance criteria as an occurrence it must NOT generate.

   brace-wrapped `-p` are real shapes, closed by plan 03-35 Task 3 and asserted by

   reason is cost — the sweep spawns one real hook process per generated variant — and it is recorded
   in the boundary document with its own reopening trigger by plan 03-35, not left as an unstated
   omission. If a future round finds a wrapper-flag spelling that is neither swept nor named in that
   test, adding the flag tokens here is the answer.

3. **The seven sibling requirement IDs are recorded, not re-marked.** The orchestrator's brief is
