<objective>
Make the project's own documents agree with each other, and with the evidence, about what this phase
achieved.

Purpose: `03-VERIFICATION.md`'s gap names two deliverables. Plan 03-20 and plan 03-21 answer the
first. This plan answers the second: a reconciled statement of AGNT-07's status. Today
`03-19-SUMMARY.md`'s frontmatter claims `AGNT-07` among its completed requirements while that same
document's key-decisions section states in plain words that AGNT-07 is not re-certified as fully
closed. A reader cannot tell which sentence to believe, and neither can a future verifier.

Output: one consistent status for AGNT-07 across `REQUIREMENTS.md` and `03-19-SUMMARY.md`, chosen
from the evidence plan 03-21 recorded; seven sibling requirement statuses aligned with
`03-VERIFICATION.md`'s own coverage table; and a `STATE.md` blocker entry that no longer describes a
finished investigation as un-investigated.
</objective>
