The four motions, playable

This page inlines the real motion.css and tokens.css. The animations are the product's own CSS; the buttons only replay them. Press each motion's button a few times, then answer one question: confirm all four, or name a different millisecond value for any of them.

Speed (all four):

1. Panel slide-in: 220ms

Easing
cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1), the one curve
Technique
transform: translateX(100%) to rest. Width is never animated.
Why this number
A peer surface arriving, not a modal. It travels the furthest (the panel's full 480px) so it gets the longest time.
Felt time
90% of the travel by 73ms, 99% by 136ms
Half / double
110ms reads as a snap; 440ms makes opening a task feel like waiting for a drawer.
Reduced motion
Zero: the panel is simply there.

2. Group expand / collapse: 180ms

Easing
the one curve for the rows below; linear per-row share for the wipe
Technique
Rows below travel by transform on the collapse duration. New rows wipe in top-down by clip-path, each an equal share of the duration. Removed rows leave at once. No height, no measurement.
Why this number
Nothing travels far; rows settle. A longer collapse reads as lag.
Half / double
90ms is close to a jump-cut; 360ms and a 20-row group is still unfolding when you reach for the next key.
Felt time
90% of the travel by 59ms, 99% by 112ms. The curve is very front-loaded, so the label overstates how long this feels.
Reduced motion
Zero: rows appear and disappear at once.

3. Palette open: 140ms

Easing
the one curve
Technique
Full opacity and a full scrim on the first frame, then a transform-only settle of 8px. Never a fade, never a scale.
Why this number
It must feel instant: you pressed a key and are already typing.
The 8px is a judgement
The contract caps the settle at 8px and says plan 13 picks the distance. Plan 09 flagged that cap as its own judgement, not an owner decision. 4px is barely visible; 16px breaks the cap.
Felt time
90% of the travel by 46ms, 99% by 87ms
Half / double
70ms is a snap with no settle; 280ms means the text field is moving while you type into it.
Reduced motion
Zero: the palette is simply there.

4. Selection move: 120ms

Easing
the one curve
Technique
The row wash (background and inset shadow) transitions. Nothing moves.
Why this number
Signals "you moved". Faster is a snap; slower makes holding the arrow key feel sticky.
Felt time
90% of the travel by 40ms, 99% by 74ms
Half / double
60ms is a snap; 240ms leaves a smear behind the cursor at key-repeat speed.
Reduced motion
Zero: the wash changes at once.

Click a row, or focus the list and press the up and down arrows. Hold the key to feel key-repeat.
