### A-01 — The epic roster becomes the home surface

**Question (verbatim from the plan's `<decision>`):** "Amend `01-UI-SPEC.md` § Information Architecture and § Surface Inventory so the epic roster, not the flat status-banded list, is the primary surface."

**Resume signal:** Reply "approve A-01" or "decline A-01", with a reason if declining.

**Options offered (2):**

1. **approve** - Approve A-01 as stated. Pro: the home surface shows progress and roadmap together; plans 11 and 12 proceed. Con: costly to unwind, because the flat rack would have to be rebuilt.
2. **decline** - Decline and keep the flat status-banded list as the primary surface. Pro: no IA change. Con: the adopted comps' home surface is void, and plans 05 and 11 lose their purpose.

**Answer:** **Approved as stated.** The owner accepted that this is an information-architecture change rather than a skin change, that the row model, the virtualizer's size function and the board query's grouping move together, and that it is costly to unwind. Unchanged: one board, one virtualized rack, one keyboard map, one query.

**Rationale given:** the owner's adoption of the direction on 2026-10-07 already implied it, since the adopted comps' home surface is the epic roster.

### A-02 — The status-band rack becomes a grouping lens

**Question (verbatim from the plan's `<decision>`):** "Close `01-UI-SPEC.md` Open Decision 9 by shipping a grouping toggle that switches the grouping key between epic and status."

**Resume signal:** Reply "approve A-02" or "decline A-02", with a reason if declining.

**Options offered (2):**

1. **approve** - Approve A-02 as stated. Pro: the cross-epic review sweep survives the move to an epic-first home; one query serves both lenses. Con: one more piece of board state to reason about.
2. **decline** - Decline and keep status as the only grouping key. Pro: nothing new to learn; Open Decision 9 stays open. Con: if A-01 was approved, the cross-epic review sweep has no home.

**Answer:** **Approved as stated.** Open Decision 9 is closed as shipped. The status grouping is the same rack with a different grouping key, not a second surface and not a second code path. **This decision grants no keybinding for the toggle.** A keybinding would be a Keyboard Map amendment and its own separate decision, which this gate did not grant.

**Rationale given:** the "No epic" bucket holds all 181 in-review tasks, the entire review queue, which would have no home once the surface is epic-first.

### A-03 — Row Anatomy gains a change-cue slot and four row-kind heights

**Question (verbatim from the plan's `<decision>`):** "Amend `01-UI-SPEC.md` § Row Anatomy twice in one decision: add the change-cue slot, and replace the single row height with four heights by row kind."

**Resume signal:** Reply "approve A-03", "approve cue only", "approve heights only", or "decline A-03".

**Options offered (4, not 2):**

1. **approve-both** - Approve A-03 in full, the cue slot and the four heights. Pro: the rack carries epic headers, challenges and tasks at readable densities, and the cue is visible where work is. Con: costly to unwind; several board tests change at once.
2. **approve-cue-only** - Approve the cue slot, decline the four heights. Pro: the cue ships without touching the rack's layout assumptions. Con: an epic header, a challenge and a task would all render at one height, which the comp does not support.
3. **approve-heights-only** - Approve the four heights, decline the cue slot. Pro: the nested rack works; Row Anatomy's field list is untouched. Con: plan 01's annotation has no sanctioned slot, and the measured UAT cost it addresses stays unfixed.
4. **decline** - Decline both. Pro: Row Anatomy stays as locked. Con: neither the roster nor the cue can ship as comped.

**Answer:** **Approved in full (option 1). Neither half was declined.** The cue slot takes the graded annotation plan 01 shipped (commits-ahead count, uncommitted-changes mark, nothing for a task with no worktree). Epic rows carry the rolled-up signal elsewhere, because the proposal-count slot is taken. The single row height becomes four: epic header ~64px, challenge ~48px, task ~32px, band header ~28px. The Performance Contract survives intact: the size function stays an O(1) lookup on row kind with no per-row measurement.

**Costs the owner accepted:** the volume fixture re-declares the row height instead of importing it; the quick-add row-index logic is only correct while backlog leads the band order; three keyboard and layer tests must be re-run.

**Rationale given:** each middle option breaks something. "Cue only" renders three row kinds at one height, which the comp cannot support. "Heights only" strands the annotation plan 01 already shipped.
