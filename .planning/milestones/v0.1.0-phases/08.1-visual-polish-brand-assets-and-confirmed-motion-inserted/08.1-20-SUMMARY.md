# Phase 08.1 Plan 20: The Phase Gate, the Design Document and the Honest Register Summary

**The gate is green on every arm (1225 jsdom, 63 + 63 + 63 WebKit, 1142 Rust, both builds); DESIGN.md now describes the dual-mode Mission Control world that shipped, with the superseded Flight Progress Strips log kept under a supersession table; and twelve new register entries (D-REV-20 to D-REV-31) queue everything this phase asserted and nobody observed, including four findings the earlier passes had not recorded.**

## Read this first: what this phase did and did not establish

Five things are true at once, and none of them is a green-tests claim.

1. **The owner's judgement, criterion 0, is of the comps.** He adopted Mission Control on 2026-10-07 after viewing six code-rendered comps. No record shows him viewing the built board. The built captures are harness pages of real components, not the app shell. D-REV-24.
2. **Seven surface groups were finished and corrected, and none has been re-reviewed after its fixes.** Six independent reviewers each returned `disposition: fix` once. Plan 21's changed foot has never been seen by one. The app shell has never been reviewed at all. D-REV-26. This executor has no subagent tool, so it could not spawn the reviewer or the documenter.
3. **Dark mode's activation in the Tauri web view is an assumption nobody has run.** The values are proven by the contrast tests, and the dark browser arm proves a WebKit engine emulating the scheme selects them. That is not the shell. D-REV-22.
4. **A-04 is not what the owner chose.** Under the has-worktree chip the epic lens shows all 13 headers, which is the `always` reading he rejected. D-REV-27.
5. **The brand is spent in more places than its rule allows,** including a 2px brand-coloured left border on `review/review.css` `.review-anchor-widget`, which A-06 does not cover. D-REV-28. This was found by this pass's grep, not by a reviewer.

## Success criteria, one by one

### Criterion 0: "The owner looks at the board with real data on it and judges it bold and modern"

**Status: closed on the owner's adoption of the direction; open as worded for the built product.**

- *Mechanical half (closed by tests):* the per-mode contrast assertions (`theme/__tests__/contrast.test.ts`, 1225 jsdom tests pass) and the painted-contrast browser tests in WebKit, both schemes (`legibility.browser.test.tsx`, run today in the default and dark arms).
- *Judged half (owner's own decision):* he ADOPTED the Mission Control direction wholesale on 2026-10-07 (`08.1-DIRECTION.md` header, the adoption line committed as `e037ea8`, recorded in `08.1-04-SUMMARY.md`), after viewing all six rendered comps. Before answering he was told that the comps were code-rendered and not image-generated, that several values were labelled FIXTURE, and that adoption was one-way. He accepted three caveats the orchestrator raised: the craft detector had not actually run (it has since, and reported 135 warnings and no errors on the comps), the roster's rhythm was untested because the seed data is uniform, and the roster's lead row is a junk epic.
- *What this does not establish:* that he has looked at the **built** board with real data. The adoption is of the comps. Every built capture is a component page; the comp's `Mission Control` masthead is not in the source. Queued as D-REV-24 (human aesthetic judgement) and D-REV-25 (the junk epic and the 181-task no-epic bucket, from plan 04's caveat c, which plan 04 asked this plan to register).

### Criterion 1: "The installed application's icon is the mark, not the scaffold, on every artifact `tauri.conf.json` lists"

**Status: closed on bundle contents. What the OS paints is not observed.**

Evidence: `theme/__tests__/icons.test.ts` (15 tests, all pass) reads every `bundle.icon` artifact and asserts it differs from the recorded scaffold blob, carries the right dimensions and colour type, and that the 32x32 has a transparent corner. This plan added a test that measures `icon.png`'s opaque artwork at 412 of 512, centred (the recorded 824-of-1024 margin was an assumption until now; I also confirmed the test fails when the expected figure is wrong). The Dock, Finder and About panel are queued as D-REV-20 (partly automatable) and the margin's look beside other icons as D-REV-21.

### Criterion 2: "The brand asset exists in the repository and any surface entitled to render it does so"

**Status: closed, with one thing to know.**

`packages/desktop/src/assets/logo.svg` is tracked and sha1-pinned by `icons.test.ts`; `logo-glyph.svg` is tracked too. `first-run.test.tsx` § "the brand mark" asserts the first-run state and the project rail each render it as a named image. **The thing to know:** after A-08(c) both surfaces draw the monochrome glyph in ink through a CSS mask (a `role="img"` span), not the full-colour mark. The owner chose that over a fifth brand use. The criterion's wording is met; the on-screen mark is the glyph.

### Criterion 3: "The panel slides in, a group animates its height, the palette fades/scales in, each collapsing to instant under `prefers-reduced-motion`"

**Status: closed on the contract's reading; the ROADMAP's literal words are not what shipped, in two clauses.**

Evidence: `motion.browser.test.tsx` passes with nonzero durations in the default arm and zero in the reduced-motion arm (63 each, today), and `motion.test.ts` pins the stylesheet to the typed mirror.

- *Panel slides in from the trailing edge:* met as worded (`translateX`, 220ms).
- *"Animates its height":* **not as worded.** The contract forbids animating height (rows declare a container query type). The shipped technique is a staggered top-down `clip-path` wipe plus row travel. Whether it reads as growth to a human eye is queued, D-REV-23.
- *"Fades/scales it in":* **not as worded.** `08.1-UI-SPEC.md` decided the palette does not fade or scale (full opacity on frame one, an 8px transform-only settle). The owner watched this on `motion.html` and confirmed the 140ms, but no record has him ruling on the ROADMAP's word. The ROADMAP text is stale against the contract; I did not edit it.
- *Reduced motion:* met, measured in a real engine.

### Criterion 4: "The motion durations are the ones a direction round actually confirmed"

**Status: closed, and it cites an observation.**

The owner CONFIRMED all four unchanged on 2026-10-08 (panel 220ms, group collapse 180ms, palette 140ms, selection 120ms, all on `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`), **having actually opened and watched `comps/motion.html`**, which inlines the shipped `motion.css`. Recorded in `08.1-UI-SPEC.md` § Motion with the date, and pinned by `motion.test.ts`. That was the point of D-21. Two limits, both already in the contract: what he watched was the shipped CSS in a page, not the packaged app; and **`--duration-settle` (180ms, the colour wash on a settling control) was not among the four he watched and remains an unreviewed assertion.**
