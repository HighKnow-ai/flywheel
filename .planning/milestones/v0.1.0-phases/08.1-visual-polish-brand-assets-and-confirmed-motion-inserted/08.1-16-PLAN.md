<objective>
Hand-finish surface group three: the review surface, where this product's core value becomes a daily
habit rather than a slogan.

Implements **D-24** at both granularities for this group, **D-25** (hand-finished, not inherited) and
the review half of **D-11** and **D-19** — the diff's row tints are the clearest case of colour as a
data channel in the whole product, and they were measured against the old grounds.

**Why the diff tints need real care.** This group is the one place the existing browser arm was built
for: its own config documents that jsdom cannot resolve the blended row tints at all, so the ratio
cannot be measured there. The added and removed tints are a legitimate data channel under D-11, but
they were derived against the old dark chrome and the old paper stock, and both grounds have changed.
Every syntax token colour has to stay legible on them in both modes, and that is a measurement in a
real engine rather than a judgement.

**And it is finished against real content.** Plan 03 seeded real acceptance criteria on every task
that has an active worktree, specifically so the criteria rail could be designed against something.
Use that data; do not finish this rail against an empty state and call it done.

This plan does not edit `App.css` — group-scoped rules go in the existing `review/review.css`, which
is the precedent the other groups copy.

from SC0–SC4, the CONTEXT decisions and `08.1-VALIDATION.md`'s requirement-to-test map.
