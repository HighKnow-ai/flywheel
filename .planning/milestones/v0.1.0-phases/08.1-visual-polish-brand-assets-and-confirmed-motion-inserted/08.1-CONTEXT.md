# Phase 8.1: Visual Polish — Brand Assets And Confirmed Motion - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-10-07
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

This phase re-opens the visual direction the owner rejected on 2026-09-10 and 2026-10-07, and is
the **only phase in this roadmap permitted to amend sections `01-UI-SPEC.md` marks LOCKED**.

It delivers five things:

1. **A new home surface.** The epic roster leads — "Mission Control" — because the owner's own
   framing is *"the Jira of AI agents… that auto-populates, and helps feature design automatically
   challenged by agents."* Epics ARE the roadmap; each carries progress, agent activity and the
   challenges agents have raised against it.
2. **A new visual world**, light and dark, replacing the Flight Progress Strips cream-strip/
   dark-rack material split and its five paper tints.
3. **The brand mark produced** — `logo.svg` does not exist and must be authored, not wired — and
   every app-icon artifact `tauri.conf.json` lists generated from it.
4. **Motion durations that were actually confirmed**, not asserted.
5. **A board cue for which items carry real changes**, with a way to reach them among 907 rows.

**Not in scope:** any capability needing new schema. Dates, milestones, Gantt, epic-to-epic
dependencies and planned-vs-actual are all roadmap-shaped ideas that need data the board does not
have — see Deferred Ideas. Everything decided below is derivable from existing tables.

**A note on how this phase was scoped.** Mid-discussion the owner rejected three list-shaped
visual directions with *"none of the suggestions are showing progress and roadmap, which is kind
of the right mix I'm looking for."* That reframed the phase from "how does a row look" to "what
does the home surface show". This is an IA change, and it is in scope only because the owner
explicitly authorised it earlier in the same session (D-02/D-03 below).

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Direction-round scope

- **D-01:** **Full re-roll.** Flight Progress Strips competes and may be beaten; it was. The owner
  supplied his own reference (a dark GitHub/Vercel-style analytics surface) and then specified the
  IA directly, which the Impeccable contract permits — a user's pick beats the dice, exactly as on
  2026-09-09 when he took `MY PICK` over the assigned roll.
  — **Reversibility:** one-way — reverting means restoring a world the owner has now rejected
  twice, and the measured palette in `01-UI-SPEC.md` is voided by D-18 regardless.

- **D-02:** **Nothing is fenced.** The round may propose changes to any section of any UI-SPEC,
  including Row Anatomy, the Keyboard Map, the Performance Contract and the Surface/State
  inventories.

- **D-03:** **Proposal is free; adoption is gated.** Visual changes are adopted wholesale.
  **Every structural change is a separate `checkpoint:decision`** the owner approves or declines.
  This is what keeps D-02 from meaning "rewrite the keyboard map". Planner: do not fold a
  structural amendment into a visual task.

- **D-04:** **Comps before build.** The 2026-09-09 round was code-led (no image generation was
  available), so the owner first saw the direction after it shipped — which is why it took a month
  and two UAT sessions to discover he disliked it. This round comps first. Its job is now to
  **render the agreed direction, not to propose alternatives**: one direction contract authored
  from this session's decisions, comped as Mission Control light, Mission Control dark, and the
  item-detail + review surfaces, at real density against the ~907-item board. The owner approves
  or sends back before any CSS is written.

### The home surface — "Mission Control"

- **D-05:** **The epic roster leads.** Each epic row carries: title and key, a progress bar, a
  task-distribution strip by status, a count of agents running on it, and a count of proposals
  waiting on the owner. Expanding an epic reveals its content in place.
  — **Reversibility:** costly — `BoardView`'s row model, the virtualizer's size function and the
  board query's grouping all change together.

- **D-06:** **Progress is tasks done/total, with criteria as a finer second number where present.**
  The database holds **four** `acceptance_criteria` rows across a 907-task board, so a
  criteria-driven bar would read 0% on every epic. Tasks-done works today on the real board with
  no backfill. An epic whose tasks carry no criteria prints **"no criteria yet"** — a true
  statement — never a 0% that lies.

- **D-07:** **The status-band rack becomes a lens, not a second surface.** ONE board, ONE
  virtualized rack, ONE keyboard map, ONE query — a grouping toggle switches the grouping key
  between **epic** and **status**. `01-UI-SPEC.md` Open Decision #9 left "Group by" as a delegated
  enhancement; this is the door it left open. The "everything in review across all epics" sweep is
  preserved as the status grouping.

- **D-08:** **An agent's challenge renders inline, above the epic's tasks.** Expanding an epic
  shows its open proposals first, then its tasks — because answering a challenge may change what
  the tasks are. **Accepting one creates a task in place**, so the roadmap grows itself. This is
  the surfacing of work Phases 5 and 6 already shipped but which currently hides in a tray and a
  triage lane behind a small count.

- **D-09:** **The "what this says" summary line is computed from board state by a fixed rule set**
  (~6 clauses: decisions pending, items reviewable, agents blocked and for how long, epics with no
  criteria). Zero cost, zero latency, cannot hallucinate. An agent-written interpretive version is
  deferred — see Deferred Ideas.

### Visual language

- **D-10:** **The owner's reference recipe, authored for light AND dark from one token set.**
  Near-neutral ground, one surface family, 1px hairline rules, no fills. Type and spacing carry
  all hierarchy. Starting values given by the owner:

  | role | light | dark |
  |---|---|---|
  | ground | `#ffffff` | `#0a0a0a` |
  | surface | `#f6f8fa` | `#0f0f0f` |
  | rule | `#d1d9e0` | `#1f1f1f` |
  | text | `#1f2328` | `#ededed` |
  | muted | `#59636e` | `#8b8b8b` |

  The current spec **structurally cannot** do this — its cream-strip-on-dark-rack split is a
  hardcoded material story, not a theme. Dual mode is new capability in the token layer.
  — **Reversibility:** costly — every contrast pair is measured twice and the contrast test
  doubles; collapsing back to one mode later is cheap, but adding the second mode later is not.

- **D-11:** **Colour appears only as a data channel.** Progress fill, `▲N` ahead, blocked, failed,
  done. Nothing else is coloured: no tinted rows, no status fills, no pills with backgrounds. The
  five paper tints of the old world are gone.

- **D-12:** **Progress gets the brand; current position gets a rule.** The progress fill carries
  `#3c64dd`'s hue — it is what the product is about, it repeats down the page as a rhythm, and as
  a non-text element it clears 3:1 on both grounds easily. Selection becomes a 2px left rule plus
  a background shift, **not** a brand colour. Rationale in the owner's terms: a brand appearing
  forty times down a page as progress reads as identity; one appearing once as a highlight reads
  as a cursor. This **supersedes** `01-UI-SPEC.md`'s rule that accent is reserved strictly for the
  current position.

- **D-13:** **Two fixed row heights, not measured virtualization.** Epic header ~64px (title + bar
  + signals), challenge ~48px, task ~32px, band header ~28px. `estimateSize` becomes a lookup on
  row kind — still O(1) per row, still no `measureElement`. The Performance Contract survives the
  move to a nested model intact; the planner must keep it that way.

- **D-14:** **The typeface is on the table.** Archivo was LOCKED 2026-09-09 but a full re-roll puts
  it in play. A replacement must satisfy the carried-over constraints: licence permits
  self-hosting, `tnum`/`lnum` GSUB features **verified present** (not assumed — the key column and
  every count depend on them), variable preferred for bundle size, not an Impeccable banned
  training-data default, and a condensed axis or cut available for stamped keys and band headers.

### The change cue

- **D-15:** **A graded annotation in the row's fields: `▲N` commits ahead of the target ref, `●`
  for uncommitted changes present, nothing at all for a task with no worktree.** Not a fifth
  alert-shaped pill beside Blocked / Needs Decision / CI-failure, and not a colour-only strip
  treatment — the spec's own rule forbids meaning resting on colour alone.

- **D-16:** **One `board_change_flags(projectId)` command, sweeping active `worktrees` rows.**
  `git_facts` deliberately caches nothing (D-75: "derive at read time, never cache a verdict"), so
  the numbers need a live read — but `worktrees.task_id` is UNIQUE and there are a handful of rows,
  not 907. The command returns `{ taskId, ahead, dirty }[]`. It is the exact structural twin of
  `board_forge_flags`: one query per board, invalidated on `board:changed`, no new table, no cached
  verdict, **never a per-row query**.

- **D-17:** **A sixth `BoardFilter` dimension, `has_worktree`**, as pure SQL (`EXISTS` on
  `worktrees` where `status = 'active'`) inside the core's single filtering query — D-03's "no
  second filtering implementation" holds. This is what fixes the measured UAT cost: finding the one
  reviewable task in 907 rows took a hand-written SQL join plus a `git merge-tree` sweep because
  the UI showed nothing. `ahead`/`dirty` **cannot** be filter dimensions — they are git facts with
  no DB column — and stay annotation only.

- **D-18:** **An epic's change signal rolls up from its child tasks.** Note for the planner: the
  epic row's `field-epic-pill` slot **already** holds the pending-proposal count from plan 05-08,
  so this needs a different slot or a combined read.

### Palette, spec and motion

- **D-19:** **The measured palette is re-derived, not recolored.** The AA ratios in
  `01-UI-SPEC.md` § "Pinned brand, and the lifts it requires" (`#7d99ff` at 5.92:1 on the rack,
  `#2d4fb8` at 4.97:1 on the strip) were computed against the cream-strip/dark-rack pairing. Both
  grounds change, so **every one of those numbers is void**. The owner's recorded rule stands: *"a
  brand that cannot be read on its own product surface has not been applied."* The pin is on the
  hue, not the hex. `theme/__tests__/contrast.test.ts` must assert the new pairs — in both modes —
  and the table is not the proof, the runnable assertion is.
  — **Reversibility:** one-way — the old ratios cannot be restored without restoring the old
  ground.

- **D-20:** **A new `08.1-UI-SPEC.md` owns the new world and carries an explicit SUPERSEDES table.**
  It voids, by name: `01-UI-SPEC.md` § Selected Direction, § Color & Token Roles, § Design System >
  Radius Language, § Motion; `02-UI-SPEC.md` § Color Additions; `03-UI-SPEC.md` § Motion Additions.
  It leaves authoritative: Row Anatomy's field semantics, the Keyboard Map, the Performance
  Contract, the Surface and State inventories, Accessibility, and the Copywriting Contract — except
  where a `checkpoint:decision` under D-03 amends one. The old files are **not** edited in place;
  the history of why the first world lost is preserved.

- **D-21:** **Motion durations are named with a reason in the contract, then confirmed live in one
  pass.** The contract states each duration and why (e.g. panel slide-in ~220ms because a peer
  surface is arriving, not a modal; group collapse ~180ms because nothing travels; palette open
  ~140ms because it must feel instant; selection move ~120ms). It is built, the owner watches all
  four once, and confirms or names a number. Criterion 4 then cites an observation, not an
  assertion — `motion.css`'s current `180ms`/`220ms` were never reviewed by anyone.

  **This is a `checkpoint:decision`, not a `checkpoint:human-verify`.** Choosing a duration is a
  choice only the owner can make; human *verification* is suspended until Phase 11 and this is not
  it. See `.claude/CLAUDE.md` § "Human verification is deferred until MVP", which suspends the
  latter and explicitly preserves the former.

### Brand asset and app icon

- **D-22:** **Author the mark once as SVG; generate every raster from it.** `logo.svg` does **not**
  exist anywhere in the repository, despite `01-UI-SPEC.md` Gate A stating the owner supplied it —
  so success criterion 2 starts at zero and this phase must PRODUCE the asset, not wire one up.
  The mark is the **HighKnow lightbulb-brain** (owner decision 2026-10-07, already recorded in
  `.planning/PROJECT.md` § Key Decisions — do not re-ask). Single path family, `currentColor` where
  possible, no embedded raster. `tauri icon` then generates `32x32`, `128x128`, `128x128@2x`,
  `icon.icns`, `icon.ico` and every `Square*Logo.png`. The same SVG renders in-app (About item,
  first-run empty state). **macOS wants the mark inset in a rounded square, not bled to the edge.**

  Every file in `packages/desktop/src-tauri/icons/` is still a `create-tauri-app` scaffold default
  dated 9 Sep 2026, so criterion 1 also starts at zero.

### Seeding

- **D-23:** **Seed real acceptance criteria on the tasks that have an active worktree** before
  comping the review surface. The criteria rail cannot be designed against emptiness, and
  `DEFERRED-REVIEW.md` D-REV-04 records this as *"not-demonstrable — a data vacuum, not a code
  defect"*. Scope is the handful of tasks with worktrees (five, during Phase 08). Criteria may be
  written directly or proposed by an agent and accepted — which is Phase 5/6's own job.
  **Also delete the two probe rows** (`test`, `hello`) typed into a composer a focus bug had stolen
  focus into. Seeding this closes D-REV-04.

### Build process

- **D-24:** **`01-UI-SPEC.md` § Build Process Requirement applies at two granularities.** The cheap
  mechanical gate — read `reference/craft-floor.md`, run `detect.mjs --json`, fix everything it
  flags — runs on **every surface** it touches. The expensive gate — screenshots into
  `.impeccable/review/` plus the `impeccable-finish-reviewer` subagent **against the approved
  comp** — runs once per coherent surface group, not eleven separate times. Suggested groups:
  board + rail · item detail + palette · review · session · settings + triage + sheets.
  `impeccable-documenter` writes `DESIGN.md` once, at the end, from the built world.

- **D-25:** **All eleven surfaces are hand-finished in this phase**, not left to inherit tokens.
  The owner chose this over the cheaper "token-deep, hand-finish the first viewport only" option.
  Planner: this is the phase's largest cost line and the honest place to report if it must be cut.

### Claude's Discretion

- The exact rule clauses in D-09's summary line, beyond the four named.
- Which row slot `▲N`/`●` occupies and where it sits on the progressive-disclosure ladder — subject
  to the constraint that it must not be the slot already holding the proposal count on epic rows.
- How a `merged`, `stale` or `abandoned` worktree reads differently from an `active` one, if at all.
- The exact surface groupings for D-24's finish-reviewer passes.
- Icon and glyph set — Lucide holds unless the round argues otherwise, and if it does, that is a
  D-03 structural decision.

## Specific Ideas

**The owner's reference image** (supplied 2026-10-07): a dark analytics surface — near-black
ground, one surface family, 1px low-contrast rules, grouped left nav with small section labels,
large title with muted subtitle, an icon tab row, and data rows built as *label + muted sub-label +
horizontal bar + value + delta*, with saturated colour used only as a data channel and a green
check marking the better side. Underneath, a **"What this says"** block giving a plain-language
read of the numbers, and a very muted footer meta line.

Three things from it are explicitly adopted:

1. **The "what this says" block** → D-09. Nothing in the existing spec has it, and for a fleet it
   is the highest-value thing on the screen.
2. **The compare-row anatomy** → D-05's epic row: title + muted epic/sub-label + progress bar +
   value + change delta.
3. **Grouped nav with section labels** → the project rail regrouping into Projects / Fleet /
   Review queue.

**On Jira**, in the owner's words: *"kind of cool for organizing information, but maybe it's not
the best in terms of agent-native workflow."* The resolution taken: keep Jira's grouped hierarchy
and metadata density, refuse its chrome — its density is borders, fills and badges-on-badges, and
D-10/D-11 give the same density through type and spacing instead.

**The stated bar:** *"modern but professional… sleek, while having impeccable UX standards for
retrieving information (understanding the status and context of a project) and working with
agents."*

**Success criterion 0 is the real test** and it is subjective by design: the owner looks at the
board with real data on it and judges it bold and modern — not, in his words on 2026-09-10, *"a
very old app."*

<deferred>
## Deferred Ideas

- **Agent-written interpretive summary.** D-09 ships the computed line; an agent reading the board
  and writing a genuine prose read of where the project stands — the thing the reference image
  actually does — is the more agent-native version. Needs a run, a cadence, a staleness marker and
  a wrongness story. Its own phase; the slot D-09 creates is the seam.
- **A timeline/Gantt reading of the roadmap.** Needs dates, epic-to-epic dependencies and
  sequencing the schema does not have. New data, therefore a new phase.
- **Planned-vs-actual and velocity.** Explicitly banned by D-12's "aggregated nowhere" rule for
  estimates; noted here only so a future phase re-argues it deliberately rather than drifting into
  it.
- **A "Needs You" ranked queue as the home surface** — decisions, reviewable diffs, challenges and
  blocked runs ordered by cost of waiting. Considered and not chosen (the owner took Mission
  Control); it would be the natural second surface if the roadmap read proves insufficient.
- **Sorting or filtering by `ahead`/`dirty`.** Impossible as SQL — they are git facts with no DB
  column. Would need a materialized column and therefore a migration.
- **Overflow handling for more than nine registered projects** — `01-UI-SPEC.md` Open Decision #10,
  still unresolved, and the rail regrouping in Specific Ideas touches the same component.
- **Collaborative rich-text editing of epic/task descriptions** (Yjs/Automerge on single fields) —
  PROJECT.md's stack notes keep this available; nothing here needs it.
