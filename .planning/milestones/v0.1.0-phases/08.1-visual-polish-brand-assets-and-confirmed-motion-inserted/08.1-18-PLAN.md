<objective>
Hand-finish surface group five: settings, triage, the authoring tray, and the published legend.

Implements **D-24** at both granularities for this group and **D-25** (hand-finished, not inherited).

**The legend is the load-bearing item here, not the settings screen.** The inherited rule is that no
mark may exist that the legend does not define — which cuts both ways. D-11 removed the five paper
tints, so their registry entries describe marks the product no longer renders; plan 01 added two
change-cue marks and amendment A-05 settled their glyphs. The legend has to end this phase as an
accurate account of exactly what the UI draws, and its test asserts exact per-channel counts, so
removing a stale entry and updating the count is one change, not two.

**No comp covers this group.** Plan 04 comped Mission Control, the item detail and the review
surface. So these surfaces are finished against `08.1-UI-SPEC.md` directly and the reviewer pass
reviews against the contract — stated explicitly, the same disclosure the old Build Process
Requirement demanded when no direction contract existed.

This plan does not edit `App.css`. Group-scoped rules go in a new `styles/admin-surfaces.css`,
imported by the roots in this group, following the `review/review.css` precedent.

Probe fallback: skipped — this phase has no REQ-IDs to probe (visible skip). `must_haves` derive
from SC0–SC4, the CONTEXT decisions and `08.1-VALIDATION.md`'s requirement-to-test map.

Purpose: the surfaces that explain the product to itself, finished and accurate.
Output: seven finished components, one group stylesheet, a legend that matches reality, one reviewer
pass.
</objective>
