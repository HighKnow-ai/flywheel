<objective>
`01-UI-SPEC.md` marks sections LOCKED, and this is the only phase in the roadmap permitted to amend
them. **D-03** sets the price: proposal is free, adoption is gated, and **every structural change is
a separate `checkpoint:decision`** the owner approves or declines. CONTEXT states the planner's
obligation in one line — *"do not fold a structural amendment into a visual task"* — so the three
structural changes that follow from the adopted direction are three gates here, and three more are
gated in plans 08 and 09.

**D-02** is why these gates exist at all: nothing was fenced, so the round was permitted to propose
changes to Row Anatomy, the Keyboard Map, the Performance Contract and the Surface and State
inventories. D-03 is what stops that permission from meaning "rewrite the keyboard map".

This plan asks three questions and writes down three answers. It changes no code and no spec file:
plan 09 folds the answers into `08.1-UI-SPEC.md`, and plans 10 and 11 build what was approved.

These are `checkpoint:decision` gates, not verification steps. `.claude/CLAUDE.md` suspends human
*verification* until Phase 11 and explicitly preserves decisions, and this phase is decision-heavy
by design.

Probe fallback: skipped — this phase has no REQ-IDs to probe (visible skip). `must_haves` derive
from SC0–SC4, the CONTEXT decisions and `08.1-VALIDATION.md`'s requirement-to-test map.

Purpose: amend a locked contract deliberately, one named section at a time, with the owner's answer
on the record.
Output: `08.1-AMENDMENTS.md` carrying three dated decisions, each naming the section it amends.
</objective>
