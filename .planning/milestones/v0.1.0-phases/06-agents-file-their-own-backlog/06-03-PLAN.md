<objective>
**As a** developer triaging what my agents found, **I want to** accept, reject, merge or defer each
proposal individually, **so that** a finding that belongs under an item I already have becomes a
note on that item, and a finding I am not ready for leaves my queue without becoming work.

Phase 5 shipped accept, reject and amend. This plan builds the two verbs PROP-06 still needs —
merge and defer — plus the accept path for a proposed *issue* that D-97's new target type requires,
plus the two bulk paths D-104 permits. All of it lands in the core's write transaction, through
canonical writers only, so D-100's source gate stays green.

Purpose: complete the four-verb vocabulary in the one layer that owns the store of record, before
any UI exists to call it.
Output: `create_issue_in_tx`, `merge_proposal`, `defer_proposal`, the two bulk functions, the
`Issue` materialise arm, and their tests.
</objective>
