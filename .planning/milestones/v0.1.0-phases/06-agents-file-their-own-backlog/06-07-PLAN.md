<objective>
**As a** developer, **I want to** work my agents' proposal queue in a view built for it — one row at a
time, with the reason it was proposed, where it came from, and whether I have seen it before —
**so that** triage is a few minutes of reading rather than a hunt through a board lane.

PROP-06 asks for a dedicated view with four verbs. D-103 explains why a board lane cannot be it:
merge needs a target picker and defer needs a recall filter, and neither fits a 32-pixel row. This
plan builds that surface as `SessionSurface.tsx`'s sibling, reusing the shipped `ProposalRow`
component and the shipped grouping logic rather than rebuilding either.

Purpose: give the four verbs a home, and put the rationale, the turn pointer, the duplicate verdict
and the limit notice in front of the person deciding.
Output: the `triage-surface` keyboard region, `ProposalRow`'s two new handlers, `TriageSurface.tsx`,
and its component tests.
</objective>
