<objective>
Gap closure for `09-VERIFICATION.md` gap 1 (register **D-REV-38**), the first two of its three `missing`
items plus the sheet signal: **PLAN-07 answers truthfully on the default path.**

The phase sells one sentence — "the user can ask why any two tasks were sequenced and get an answer"
(ROADMAP success criterion 2, PLAN-07, D-157). The CR-02 fix made `readiness` hold an undeclared task
back (D-150: no declaration is no disjointness claim, so it never shares a wave). Nothing taught `why`
about that cause. So for a QuickAdd epic — which declares nothing, the commonest case there is — the
user asks "why is FLY-2 waiting?" and is told *"FLY-1 and FLY-2 declare disjoint sets, so neither waits
for the other"*: the exact opposite of what the dispatcher just did. The verification calls this a
regression created by the CR-02 fix, and it is: the fix made the old answer wrong.

Purpose: an explanation that contradicts the machine is worse than no explanation, because the user
acts on it. D-149 compounds it — the one human look at the draft happens without the reviewer being
told which tasks have no declared set at all, and a wrong or missing file-set is the single error that
silently voids the whole parallelism guarantee.

Output: a runs-alone cause in the core, its sentence in `dispatch_why`, and the signal on the wave plan
sheet. No new command, no new DTO field, no migration.

**Not in this plan, deliberately:** the widen-edge record (plan 09-11 — gap 1's third `missing` item),
and anything to do with the launch-path invariant (plans 09-12 and 09-13 — gap 2). Also out of scope
per the phase's scope fence: D-REV-39, D-REV-41, D-REV-42 and D-REV-32 through D-REV-36.
</objective>
