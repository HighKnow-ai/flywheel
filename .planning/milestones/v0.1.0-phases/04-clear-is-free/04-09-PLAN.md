<objective>
**As a** developer about to spend tokens on a fresh session, **I want to** see the whole briefing before
I press Enter — the prior summaries, where compaction begins, and the real state of the worktree — **so
that** I can correct it rather than discover the problem in the diff.

D-27 made the launch sheet a briefing gate rather than a confirmation dialog, and D-67 makes it the
landing surface for the phase's flagship action. This plan fills it: prior sessions with the folded and
verbatim regions visually separated, the boundary marker CTX-05 requires, and the diff-so-far in its two
labelled halves. `RunsSection.tsx` gains the per-run summary card so a summary is also readable from the
item detail panel, closing CONTEXT.md's open discretion item about where summaries render.

Purpose: make CTX-05's boundary and CTX-03's diff observable by the developer, not only by the agent.

**Constraints carried forward.** The sheet must still write nothing when it opens — `previewContextPacket`
is the only call it issues before Start Work, and that property is what makes opening and cancelling the
sheet free. And WINDOWS.md row 4 records an existing colour-hex violation in `ItemDetailPanel.tsx`; do
not add another. Every new element styles from design tokens.
</objective>
