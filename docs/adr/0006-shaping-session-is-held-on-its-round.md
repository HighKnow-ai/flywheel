# A shaping session is held on its Round

Decided in [Challenge the spec in a Round answered in the dock](https://github.com/HighKnow-ai/flywheel/issues/123) (PR [#139](https://github.com/HighKnow-ai/flywheel/pull/139)), with resumption in [#124](https://github.com/HighKnow-ai/flywheel/issues/124) (PR [#141](https://github.com/HighKnow-ai/flywheel/pull/141)) and Stage closing in [#126](https://github.com/HighKnow-ai/flywheel/issues/126) (PR [#143](https://github.com/HighKnow-ai/flywheel/pull/143)). A shaping agent posts a Round with `round_post`, and that MCP call is held, unanswered, until Cyril has answered every question in the Round. The call's request id is stored on the Round (`rounds.held_request_id`), and the last answer releases it with every answer in order. `round_wait` holds a session the same way: on an open Round it took over, and on a roadmap Round until every Proposal is decided. Because Claude Code aborts an idle MCP call after 30 minutes, every session kind is spawned with `CLAUDE_CODE_MCP_TOOL_IDLE_TIMEOUT` and `MCP_TOOL_TIMEOUT` raised to 7 days (`MCP_TOOL_TIMEOUT_MS` in `domain/session_kind.rs`). A live session held on a Round is what shows Waiting on you.

We rejected a fresh session per Round, where the agent posts a Round and exits, and Flywheel starts a new session from a packet once the Round is answered. Grilling is one line of reasoning built over several Rounds. A held session keeps the code it read and why it asked each question in its context, and it carries on from the answers without re-reading the repo. A fresh session per Round would pay for a full packet and a cold read on every Round, and it would lose whatever the agent had not yet written to the board. The cost is that a session can sit open for days, and Flywheel has to handle a session that dies while held.

## Consequences

- A Round outlives its session. Every answer is written to the board when it is given, so a dead session (killed, refused, app quit) loses nothing. **Continue shaping** starts a fresh session whose packet carries every Round with its answers and tells it to call `round_wait`. That call takes the open Round over (`rounds.session_id` and `held_request_id` are rewritten, with a `round.resumed` event).
- `round_post` is refused while a Round of the Stage is open, and the refusal names `round_wait`. `round_wait` on an answered Round is answered at once.
- Waiting on you for a Round is derived once (`query::round_waiting_on_you!()`: open, held, its session live). An open Round whose session is dead reads "Shaping Round waiting" instead, with Continue shaping.
- One session works one Stage. Closing a Stage is Cyril's click (`stage::close_stage`, human-only, refused while any question or Proposal in it is open). In one transaction it closes the Stage, opens the next and interrupts the live session, then starts the next Stage's session from a fresh packet that names that Stage. No Stage advances on its own, and no held call outlives its Stage, because an open Round always has an unanswered question that blocks the close.
